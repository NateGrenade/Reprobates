**Setting**
Reprobates is set in a futuristic, near-cyberpunk universe where most technology is magic-powered, alongside minor conventional electronics.

**Atheri**
The Atheri are an immortal race; Elion is an engineered member of it. Revenant genocided nearly all of the Atheri because he believed their immortality bred cruelty.

**Aurelia**
Aurelia is the home planet of Elion, Soren, and Chara. It is destroyed in Arc 2, which expands the scope of the setting beyond it.

**The Relic Hunters**
The Relic Hunters' Society is a religious/pirate faction that worships ancient Atheri magic. They excavate relics while blending into local populations, valuing relics over lives. They serve as the antagonist faction for the first three major arcs. Their Arc 1 leaders are Dioikitis (a pragmatist who views relics as harvestable resources) and Michani (a tech/analyst who humiliates Elion using combat data).

**Scope**
Arc 6 centers on a galactic civil war incited by Elion's own mistakes, which he ultimately fails to stop by the series' end. This war stems directly from the power vacuum left after Revenant's death: his imposed peace (maintained partly through mutually-assured-destruction-style leverage) collapses into faction warfare once he's gone.
