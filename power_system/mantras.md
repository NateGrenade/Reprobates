
Mantras are the result of binding a seal directly to the soul. To be able to do this, a Wielder must "Be Called" first. Once this happens, a Wielder essentially takes the proverbial next step in their power journey, and gains access to the Mantra.

Mantras are not just "free upgrades" for seals. The soul has a limited amount of space for mantras to bind themselves too, firstly. A stronger person can hold a handful, while a typical person cannot even hold one. Even if someone can hold a mantra on their soul, they need to be careful, because mantras are double edged swords.

A mantra isn't a one to one clone power-wise of it's original seal. The power is modified based on the content of a person's soul - unique to each individual. If someone is uniquely greedy, the seal could be altered as such during its transformation into a mantra (a fire generating seal could be modified to require sucking the heat out of an area in exchange for massively increasing it's output, embodying the idea of "greed" in a loose sense). The mantra's power isn't only affected by these traits, but the nature of the seal's effect on the user, too. Seals are tied to the soul, and thus they have simbiotic influence on one another. A mantra born from greed will try to bekon the Wielder towards embracing that greed a more. The same goes for any number of traits.

All mantras will try to latch onto negative traits by their very nature of being tied to the soul. In a way they're like a cancer: always wanting to consum more of you by feeding off of your most volatile and exploitable aspects.

Giving into these negative traits and influence of the mantra will slowly let it eat away at your mind and soul, and if unchecked, reduce you to the lowest form of those base desires and traits. This makes overusing a mantra for almost anyone a major risk and danger.


What mantras risk, they gain back in a few ways. As I already suggested, seals are modified after being fused to the soul as a Mantra. This modification is almost always strengthens the power in some way. Mantras of 'x' tier will almost always outperform a seal of that same tier. Mantras don't get an equal boost for all people, however. The more a mantra has to latch onto inside of a person (positive or negative, though negative is highly biased towards being present in almost all people), the more power it gains. The more powerful the seal, the more powerful the resulting mantra is as well, of course. And lastly, mantras are affected by the underlying strength of the soul, too. A stronger Wielder will always produce stronger mantras, and a sufficiently weak Wielder couldn't bind a very high level seal to themselves in the first place.


Mantras also retain the inverse effect capabilities of their seal counterpart, but without risking burning out or unraveling at all. The same goes for normal activation: mantras will never burn out or be limited in how much Ki they can be activated with. They're only limit is the user's Ki reserves and discretion. That is in part what makes mantras so scary.
