This is Nathan's personal framework for calibrating "adaptation"-type powers (characters who grow stronger or gain new abilities by adapting to an opponent mid-fight), used specifically to work out the exact rules and limits of Elion's ability in depth. Unlike most other abilities in Reprobates, which can be worked out more loosely over time, this one needed a firm internal logic.

**The Five Levels**
- Level 1 — Intellect/battle-IQ adaptation (e.g. Shikamaru, Sasuke vs. Deidara). Only counts as "earned" if the character's deductions are inferable from information already given to the audience (contrasted with Near in Death Note as an unearned example).
- Level 2 — Adapting to movements/attacks in real time (e.g. Sasuke's Sharingan precognition), requiring physical coordination beyond pure intellect.
- Level 3 — Supernaturally mimicking or matching physical techniques and power, but with clear limits (e.g. Garou from One Punch Man, who still couldn't match Orochi). Learning or creating techniques on the fly is allowed here, but only within what the character could theoretically achieve through realistic training.
- Level 4 — Arbitrary total power-cliffing with vague, plot-driven limits (e.g. Broly in Dragon Ball Super: Broly, and late-stage Garou) — considered more forgivable and thematic than Level 5.
- Level 5 — Limitless "pure BS" adaptation with no coherent internal logic (e.g. Mahoraga from Jujutsu Kaisen), criticized for having no real limiting principle. This grants abilities the character could never obtain through any amount of training, which is what makes Level 3 feel earned/viable by contrast and Level 5 feel untenable.

**Elion's Ceiling**
Elion's power ceiling is Level 4, not Level 5 — Level 5 was rejected as too much "BS" and narratively deflating, since it makes victory feel guaranteed even at a cost, whereas Level 4 still leaves him able to lose even at max output, preserving tension.

**How Power Level Maps to Adaptation Level in a Fight**
Elion's ability works like a voltage/overclocking analogy rather than a binary on/off:
- Low power: Levels 1 and 2 fully available (rapid skill acquisition, enhanced analysis, faster reactions/pattern recognition), negligible/inconsequential Level 4.
- Mid-to-upper power: Levels 1 and 2, some Level 3, and minor Level 4.
- Maximum sustained power: Level 3 and notable Level 4 — though sustained maximum pain and damage degrades his analytical ability over time, causing Level 3 use to fade and Level 4 to become predominant the longer he holds it.
- Losing control / overclocking past his limit: pushes him to full Level 4, with major consequences.

**Example Test Cases**
Two hypothetical opponents were used to pressure-test how Elion's Level 3 adaptation should respond: one with a straightforward lightning-based ability, and one whose Ki output is normally suppressed to 10% but rises as a music playlist builds to a crescendo, syncing Ki flow to the song's frequency to amplify it (a Ki-manipulation/mastery technique, not a seal, mantra, or intrinsic ability). The intended response for Elion is learnable, explainable Ki-manipulation counters — disrupting, mimicking, or inverting an opponent's Ki frequency on contact — rather than a Level 5 "new ability with no explanation" response. Forcing a complex/technical adaptation like this should require Elion to intentionally flare his power intensely, at high cost, to figure it out quickly, and he should remain imperfect at it for most or all of the fight rather than mastering it within a few tries, to avoid the payoff feeling contrived.

**Permanence**
In-fight power growth is not permanent — Elion's adapted body/abilities are sustained only by active Ki/Rune Eyes output during combat and regress after the fight ends, both to avoid him becoming drastically stronger after every fight and to preserve in-lore logic. However, each meaningful adaptation does leave a small amount of permanent, compounding strength growth, mirroring how each use also leaves a small amount of permanent, compounding pain.
