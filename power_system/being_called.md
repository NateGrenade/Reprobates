"Being Called" (originally drafted as "Awakening") is a prerequisite all Wielders must undergo to raise their Ki output and technique mastery, and to gain the understanding needed to bind a seal to their soul as a Mantra.

**Trigger**
The current model: Being Called is triggered by facing near-certain death, where the person must find a reason to keep living beyond mere instinctive self-preservation or comfort-seeking. This reframes it as the doorway into a character's ideological development — prompting them to keep asking "why" until true ideals form — rather than the culmination of that development. Full Rune Eyes evolution (see power_system/rune-eyes.md) is reserved for the moment an ideology is cemented irrevocably.


**Atheri and Timing**
Atheri, including Elion and Soren, have a significantly heightened innate disposition toward Being Called, which is why it happens for them so young (early 20s). Other, less inherently powerful characters need to work harder and longer to reach the same point.

**Naming**
"Being Called" was chosen over "Awakening" and "The Threshold" — the name is meant to evoke both a calling to life and a calling toward one's eventual ideals and convictions.
