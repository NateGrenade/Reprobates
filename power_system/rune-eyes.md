Rune Eyes are a lineage ability carried by Elion and Soren, distinct from the seal/mantra system described elsewhere in power_system.

**Origin**
Rune Eyes were originally manifested by an ancestor named Formidos, in a failed, desperate attempt to cure death.

**Core Mechanic**
Rune Eyes fulfill the user's deepest desire through raw power, but imperfectly. Formidos, for example, gained an immense lifespan but not true immortality. More generally, Rune Eyes amplify whatever self-focused desire drives the user most strongly.

**Visual Progression**
As a user's ideological conviction hardens, glowing cracks propagate from the sclera down the neck and body, eventually meeting at the heart — described as the "point of no return."

**Overuse and Burnout**
Pushing Rune Eyes past their safe upper limit causes the user's body to burn/cook itself from the Ki concentration. Afterward, the eye circuit locks up and turns charcoal-colored, causing total spiritual blockage and a temporary loss of lineage traits like regeneration and longevity. This lockout can last for days and is worse and longer for whoever exceeded the limit more severely — comparable to a seal burning out.

**Loss of Control**
Users can push Rune Eyes into a rage- or hate-fueled overdrive state, where they aren't fully themselves. Partial lucidity can still be present in this state (Elion parries Soren's final attack in their fight despite being in this state); full loss of self is also possible, and is more destructive.
