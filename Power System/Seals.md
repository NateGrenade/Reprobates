These are the standard apparatuses through which magic is activated in the Reprobates universe. Seals are tattoo-like, physical constructs woven together that produce an effect when [[Lattice|Ki]] flows through them. Seals have a standard direction which they expect Ki to flow through them, in the same way that water is expected to flow up through a pipe and out of the faucet head.

These effects can be a multitude of things: they could produce something (fire, water, etc.), they could change the user somehow (e.g. making their skin tougher), they could affect an area (reduce gravity in an area, for example), or so on. Seals do have constraints though. They are graded on a 4-tier scale: "Simple", "Perfect", "Transcendent", "Sublime".

Seals have strict limits to how much Ki can be poured into them at a time. While a seal could activate at a low output from just a little bit of Ki, there is an upper bound that cannot be exceeded without damaging or totally unraveling a seal. Seals can also suffer "burnout" from overuse at maximum or near-maximum output for a sustained time.

Seals, being physical tattoo-like structures, can be bound to many things. This includes scrolls, items, people, etc. They can be transferred to different people, meaning some people hunt for people carrying powerful seals to kill them and obtain the seal. Seals could be handed down from generation to generation in a family, or even given out to top warriors in a faction/organization. This makes carrying highly sought after or powerful seals a risk, in some sense. 


If a seal is embedded in an object with enough precision, that object becomes a **mechanism**: magical feats combined with technical ones. **Relics** are ancient mechanisms whose technology was lost over time. A mechanism becomes a relic once it's lost; there's no functional difference between them, only time and myth. If a person binds a seal to their very soul, that becomes a "[[Mantras|mantra]]".


Lastly, someone who has enough proficiency with a particular seal can learn to push Ki through it in the opposite direction. Doing this properly causes the seal to generate an inverse effect, either in concept or result, to the normal ability of the seal. For example, a seal that generates fire could have an inverse ability of absorbing/dissipating heat. Overusing this ability won't just burn out the seal, but it risks unraveling it entirely. 

## Weavers, transplanting, and overfilling
- Seals are woven by **weavers**. A complex seal (say, "lasers that shoot more lasers") needs a skilled one.
- Seals on the body draw Ki from the user's Ki networks (see [[Lattice#Ki networks (Ki channels)]]).
- **Transplanting:** a seal can be pried off one person's skin and placed on another's. It then has to **bind**, which takes time: it steams and mildly burns as it binds, and can't be used until it finishes. A strong enough power (e.g. Elion's awakening in Book 1) can massively accelerate binding.
- **Overfilling:** pouring in Ki past saturation makes a seal physically bulge as it strains to hold together. Pushed further, its effect erupts at far greater intensity (Elion's stolen water seal fires a razor-thin jet). This is the upper bound from above in action, with the same risk of damaging or unraveling the seal.
