## What it is
Forced Ki Assimilation (FKA) means shifting [[Lattice|Ki]] through the body and into the muscles, increasing power several times over for a short burst. It needs no seal or mantra. Unlike an [[Innate Techniques|innate technique]], anyone can learn it at any point in life, but it rewards high Ki control. The sensation is a searing, sparking pain in the limb, which the user has to focus on and push through rather than flinch from. It can be sustained, with continual strain on the body.

## Risk
- For most people it's dangerous. It can fry the user's Ki networks or even kill them. Overuse doesn't just burn out the Ki networks temporarily: it can **permanently eliminate** the user's control of and access to Ki.
- This is separate from ordinary burnout ([[Lattice]]): burnout comes from general overdraw and recovers in days or weeks. FKA is a more visceral application directly on the body, with harsher drawbacks.
- [[Elion]] and [[Soren]] can use it in their bodies because, for whatever reason, they handle it well. Their instructor called Soren's tolerance "a total anomaly, not an invitation."

## Users
- **Soren** taught himself FKA as a child, around 11. It made him a "prodigy" who could crater stone at the family demonstrations.
- **Elion** forced himself to learn it at 11, training in secret for weeks, and at the next demonstration matched Soren's crater, breaking his hand (see [[Elion#Childhood]]). By Book 1 both brothers use it routinely, e.g. to blast open sanctuary doors.
- **Aeros** is taught it by Elion in Book 1, built on his Ki sensitivity, with a warning to use it only when strictly necessary. He pulls it off once to save Evelyn, shattering his leg (see [[Supporting Cast#Evelyn & Aeros]]).
