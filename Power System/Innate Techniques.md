## Innate techniques
An innate technique is direct access to a supernatural power tied to [[Lattice|Ki]] that needs no outside thing ([[Seals|seal]] or [[Mantras|mantra]]) to channel or weave Ki for it to work. It is inherent and rare: the vast majority of people have none. Learnable seal-less techniques like [[Forced Ki Assimilation]] are an adjacent category; anyone can pick those up.

## Sensitives
Sensitives have an innate ability to sense Ki: its flow, density, and sources, including the Ki radiating off people.
- Aeros feels Ki as **warmth** (ambient Ki as faint warmth, dense flows like a warm bath or current, a concentrated point like a hand over fire, an incoming attack as a hot spot on the skin). Other Sensitives may perceive it differently.
- Honing the sense also trains Ki control, which makes Sensitives strong candidates for techniques like Forced Ki Assimilation.
- **Saturation:** when baseline Ki is extremely high (e.g. around [[The Obelisk]]), a Sensitive can't tell differences in flow or density, like asking which part of boiling water is hottest. Strong Sensitives adjust after a moment.
- The [[Relic Hunters' Society]] identifies Sensitives through lineage, tracks them, and conscripts them into survey work (see [[Book 1 Plot Holes and Fixes]] §1).
- Known Sensitives: Evelyn and Aeros (see [[Supporting Cast#Evelyn & Aeros]]). Aeros is the most gifted the RHS has tested.
