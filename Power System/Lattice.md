The Lattice is a network of Ki connections and threads that connect all things in the universe to one another. It is through this that Wielders are able to channel Ki into themselves and use it. People do not innately possess or create their own Ki.

This raises a simple question: if The Lattice is akin to an unending river of Ki, why doesn't everyone have effectively infinite Ki at their disposal? The answer is that people can only handle a certain amount of Ki flowing in/through them at a time. Think of a pipe beneath a river: despite the river supplying a huge amount of water, the pipe is only so wide, limiting the flow of water through it at any one time.

As people channel Ki through themselves, their body and Ki channels become stressed over time. One can push past this exhaustion through willpower or necessity, but at a certain point, a person will completely burn out - similar to a [[Seals|seal]] burning out. When this happens, it can be days or even weeks before they can channel Ki through/into themselves again, even if their physical body heals completely.

## Ki networks (Ki channels)
The internal network through which Ki travels in the body ("Ki networks" and "Ki channels" are the same thing).
- Seals on the body link to the Ki networks, which pool Ki into them and saturate them to activate them.
- Mantras are bound to the soul, and the Ki networks activate them there the same way.
- The networks can also discharge Ki into external things, like a seal on an object or a mechanism.

## Ki gateways
The gateway is what lets Ki flow into the body from the Lattice. The body stores Ki and lets it flow through, but the supply isn't infinite.
- How much Ki a person can let in depends on their gateway, which closes as they force more through it, mirroring their exhaustion.
- A user can meter their draw, closing the gateway slowly, or force in as much as possible at once, closing it proportionally faster.
- When the gateway shuts, the user is cut off from Ki for the time being (Soren after his first Rune Eyes fight in Book 1).

**Open:** how overdraw and burnout fit the gateway model. One idea: overdraw is forcing the gateway back open after it should close, which burns the Ki networks for a long time depending on severity. Not settled. ([[Forced Ki Assimilation]] carries its own, separate permanent-damage risk.)
