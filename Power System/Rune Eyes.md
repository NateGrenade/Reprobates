Rune Eyes are a lineage ability carried by [[Elion]] and [[Soren]], distinct from the [[Seals|seal]]/[[Mantras|mantra]] system described elsewhere in power_system.

## Origin
Rune Eyes were originally manifested by an ancestor named Formidos, in a failed, desperate attempt to cure death.

## Core Mechanic
Rune Eyes fulfill the user's deepest desire through raw power, but imperfectly. Formidos, for example, gained an immense lifespan but not true immortality. More generally, Rune Eyes amplify whatever self-focused desire drives the user most strongly.

## Awakening and activation
- Rune Eyes manifest when the user awakens (see [[Being Called]]). **Soren** awakens fully in Book 1 (Maristadiz). **Elion** only **half-awakens** in Book 1 (in the Obelisk cavern) and fully awakens late in Book 2.
- A half-awakening is unstable: the irises fight to glow and can't hold their luminescence for more than moments, and the sclera crack, shine, and re-seal in a continuous struggle, mirroring a mind whose thoughts and fears battle for dominance.
- Colors so far: Soren's are purple (glowing irises, pinpoint pupils, glowing cracks across the sclera, purple Ki); Elion's are gold.
- Awakening comes with a surge of Ki and rapid healing (Soren's cracked skull heals almost instantly, steaming).
- Users can run the eyes partially (extra strength, speed, Ki, and sight) or fully activate them to use their strongest asset (Soren's absorption).
- Awakenings have consequences beyond the user: see [[Structure and Themes#Trilogy One and the Aioni thread]].

## Strength and conviction
The eyes' strength increases massively as the user's convictions harden. What the twins show in Book 1 is great relative to anything seen so far, but laughable next to cosmic-scale conflicts.

## Lattice sight
Rune Eyes let the user see [[Lattice|the Lattice]], the framework of Ki that extends everywhere, along with people's connections to it. Because everyone is connected, the user can track people by how their connections shift. Both twins have this: Soren tracks enemies through trees and locates Elion in the caves, and Elion senses Soren approaching underground.

## Individual manifestations
Each user's eyes express their own desire through one underlying power, and specific abilities emerge from it rather than being separate eye powers:
- **Soren:** absorption. See [[Soren#Absorption]].
- **Elion:** adaptation. Learning an opponent's patterns or growing resistance to electricity are adaptations, not distinct powers. See [[Elion#Adaptation Power]].

## Visual Progression
As a user's ideological conviction hardens, glowing cracks propagate from the sclera down the neck and body, eventually meeting at the heart — described as the "point of no return."

## Overuse and Burnout
For unpracticed users, the main drawback is stamina: the eyes pull an enormous amount of Ki very quickly and drain the body fast.

Pushing Rune Eyes past their safe upper limit causes the user's body immense strain. For users who have not mastered the eyes, and thus cannot have them perpetually manifested, the eye circuit locks up and the user cannot manifest their Rune Eyes for a long period, causing total spiritual blockage and a temporary loss of lineage traits like regeneration and longevity. This lockout can last for days and is worse and longer for whoever exceeded the limit more severely — comparable to a seal burning out.

Visibly, the eyes revert to normal and the cracks recede as the user's Ki gateway shuts (see [[Lattice#Ki gateways]]). Soren after his first fight in Book 1 is the example. Elion's charred skin is specific to him and to his adaptation, not a general Rune Eyes effect (see [[Elion#Rune Eyes]]).

## Loss of Control
Some users can push Rune Eyes into a rage- or hate-fueled overdrive state, where they aren't fully themselves. Partial lucidity can still be present in this state (Elion parries Soren's final attack in their fight despite being in this state).
