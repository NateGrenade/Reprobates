## Aim
Give each form of magic a different job and a different cost, plus a few rules that bind all of them, so the system forces creativity instead of allowing any power.

## Rules
1. **Specificity.** The narrower an effect, the more it gets per unit of [[Lattice|Ki]] and the higher its ceiling. "Control water" is weak at everything; "fire a razor-thin jet from the palm" is strong. Weavers and users have to commit. This mirrors Elion's adaptation, which grows stronger with a focused goal.
2. **Nothing from nothing.** Ki moves, shapes, and strengthens what already exists cheaply; making something from nothing is expensive. A water seal is strong at a river and weak in a desert. Fits the existing inverse example (fire ↔ absorbing heat).
3. **The body is the bottleneck.** [[Seals]] on the body run through the bearer's Ki networks, so output is capped by the person, not just the seal, and several active seals compete for that flow. What a person carries is a real choice, the way soul space limits [[Mantras|mantras]].
4. **Hard limits.** No seal creates Ki, raises the dead (only the [[Aethercrux]] does), or touches a soul. Affecting minds at scale takes relic-grade work, which is why only the [[Relic Hunters' Society]] can run its control network.

## Division of labor
| Form | What it's for | What it costs |
|---|---|---|
| Seal | Versatile and steerable: the user aims and shapes it, and can use the inverse | Capped by the bearer's channels; can be stolen by killing the bearer |
| [[Mechanisms\|Mechanism]] | The object's design does the shaping, so it outputs more than a body could (the flamethrower's nozzle concentrates a modest fire seal). Little skill needed. Can hold seals too strong for a body. Can run on runes instead of a person | One rigid function, no inverse, breakable, can be taken, needs a weaver and a craftsman |
| [[Runes\|Rune]] | The plumbing: no weaver needed, anyone trained can draw one | Generates nothing; temporary |
| Mantra | Personal, with no limit per activation | Corruption and soul space |
| Relic | A mechanism built by people who understood these pairings far better than today's weavers, which is why relics outclass anything modern | Lost knowledge |

## Story consequences
- If runes generate nothing and minds need relic-grade work, the RHS's complacency network must have a **source**, likely a relic powering it somewhere. A physical target for later books.
- [[Rune Eyes]]' Lattice sight could be why [[Elion]] and [[Soren]] can see the network or shrug it off.
