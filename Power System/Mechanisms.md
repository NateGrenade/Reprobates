A mechanism is an object imbued with a [[Seals|seal]] that works synergistically with the item itself: magical feats combined with technical ones. The function of the item must coincide with the function of the seal placed on it, and the seal must be embedded with enough precision.

## Relics
Relics are mechanisms of old whose technology was lost over time, such as [[The Obelisk]] and the [[Aethercrux]]. A mechanism becomes a relic once it's lost. There's no functional difference between them, only time and myth. [[Titans]] are amalgamations of relics and old technology.

## Examples
- RHS gear: a flail with an extending chain, and Michani's seal-fed flamethrower.
