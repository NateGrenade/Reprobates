Runes are pure [[Lattice|Ki]] sketched into or onto something. Unlike [[Seals|seals]], they typically channel, modify, or amplify an existing ability rather than generating something new.

- **Energizing Ki runes** are a simple form: they channel a certain amount of Ki from the Lattice. Drawn in a circle, they activate [[The Obelisk]].
- The [[Relic Hunters' Society]] lays runic symbols across [[The Concord]] and the world. The magic that keeps people complacent flows through them (see [[Relic Hunters' Society#Grip on Aurelia]]).
- Proposed limits (no weaver needed, temporary, generate nothing): see [[Magic Limits]].
