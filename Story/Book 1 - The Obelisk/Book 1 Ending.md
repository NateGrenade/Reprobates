# Book 1 Ending

Beat summary of the Drive v1 draft (Climax → End). This replaces the earlier ending (Ki amalgamations swarming the cave, Elion one-shotting Michani and beating Dioikitis with a single strike, Soren dragging everyone to safety, the hospital/party "happy ending," and the Legion epilogue), which v1 marks deprecated. The draft ending isn't finished yet; open pieces are listed at the bottom.

## Climax
1. **Soren's stand.** [[Soren]] holds the forest against [[Supporting Cast#Dioikitis & Michani|Dioikitis]], Michani, and three elite soldiers. Dioikitis orders his men in a non-Concordian tongue.
   - Through his eyes Soren sees [[Lattice|the Lattice]] and tracks his enemies by it. He absorbs Michani's flamethrower and spears Dioikitis's arm with Spearing Thunder.
   - Overcharged plasma volleys wear him down until his eyes fail. He shifts from winning to inflicting damage: a final Spearing Thunder punches through Dioikitis's armor, ribs, and right lung.
   - He slips away to find Elion.
2. **The tunnels.** Two elite soldiers (an invisible fighter and a plasma gunner) pursue. Elion hesitates mid-strike (remembering someone begging for mercy) and takes plasma. The tracker shocks him into a seizure.
   - Aeros comes back to help over Evelyn's objection ("I refuse to be just 'a child'"), and Evelyn follows.
   - Evelyn binds and chokes out the gunner, and Elion headbutts the invisible soldier unconscious.
3. **The cavern.** At a dead end, Elion breaks through with repeated Forced Ki Assimilation, shocked each time, into the [[The Obelisk|Obelisk]] cavern: rivers of Ki, the Obelisk floating against the ceiling.
   - Dioikitis and Michani fly in by jet.
   - Dioikitis begins drawing energizing Ki runes to heal himself. Michani toys with the others ("Careful there Eli…").
4. **The Heart Gem.** Elion abandons the others to climb the Obelisk and wrench out the Heart Gem: he can save Chara, be called a hero, and hand his parents a priceless artifact.
   - Michani brutalizes Evelyn, and her overloaded suit detonates.
   - Aeros pulls off Forced Ki Assimilation to shove her clear, shattering his leg, and takes Michani's blade through the neck. He is **near-dead**, not dead.
   - Elion keeps striking the gem (*"Aeros is already gone!"*), then leaps down onto Michani in rage.
5. **"Two sides of a coin."** Michani chokes Elion and names him as his mirror: someone who does what he wants because he can, and who abandoned the people who brought him here.
   - Elion deliberately crams Ki into the tracker. The discharge scrambles Michani's systems.
   - Elion throws Aeros's body into Dioikitis's rune circle, knocking Dioikitis out of it. The Obelisk fires and heals Aeros, who wakes unable to feel Ki.
   - Evelyn chokes Michani, but he doesn't need air; he's rebooting.
6. **The Ki river.** Dioikitis blasts a hole through Elion's heart, and he falls into a Ki river. Evelyn flees with Aeros ("He's getting what he deserves"); they pass Soren limping down: "You need to find somewhere safe."
   - In the river: total agony. The tracker detonates. He remembers Chara praising his "trying."
   - *"I can't let myself be weak… Since when does trying matter."*
   - Meanwhile Dioikitis restarts the runes and begins healing.
7. **Half-awakening.** The river turns gold and explodes, cancelling the ritual and dropping a half-healed Dioikitis.
   - Elion's wounds knit. His gold eyes flicker, and his sclera crack and re-seal as his thoughts battle for dominance. This is only a **half-awakening** (see [[Elion#Rune Eyes]]).
   - Dioikitis: "There's two? That's not possible."
   - Michani laughs ("I haven't had this kind of fun in decades… To think that there's two!"), refuses to help, dodges Dioikitis's shot, and flies off.
8. **Elion vs. Dioikitis.** Elion can't absorb Spearing Thunder, and Dioikitis outfights him in an overclocked suit.
   - Elion adapts: he learns Dioikitis's patterns until he's moving first, and forms miniature Ki lattices under his skin that shrug off electricity. His white-gold aura burns him, and as he pushes too far it goes haywire and chars his skin.
   - Sensing Soren's approach through the Lattice, he decides the goal is saving Chara, not winning.
   - His power forces the stolen seal to finish binding. He overfills it and fires a razor-thin water jet that cuts Dioikitis's half-healed ribs.
   - With Dioikitis's max-power plasma bolt inbound, Elion blacks out: *"It's up to you, Sor—"*

## End
- Elion wakes after just over two weeks in the manor infirmary and runs to Chara's room. Her face hasn't the slightest mark: she's healed, running around outside, and the public is excited to see her.
- Their parents threw her a party. Chara asks: *"Did you see Soren's crazy weird eyes?"* So Soren's eyes are known at home, an intentional leak (people know he has wild powers, not what he is).
- Placeholder in the draft, to be added: Chara thanks Elion ("You saved me. You're amazing!"), and he answers, "I didn't do as much as you think…"

## Planned (not yet in draft)
- **Dioikitis survives,** is imprisoned by Soren, and has his gear and database copy destroyed. Under interrogation he breaks down, then is killed by a silencing seal the moment he tries to reveal the truth. (Full beat: [[Supporting Cast#Dioikitis & Michani]].)
- **Aeros's Ki sensitivity** returns only over Book 2 and much of Book 3.
- **Legion:** the old deep-space epilogue moves to Book 2, and Legion himself arrives at the end of Book 3 (see [[Structure and Themes#Trilogy One and the Aioni thread]]).
