# Book 1 Middle

Beat summary of the Drive v1 draft (Early Middle → Middle Middle → End of Middle). The overall structure is likely to hold, but expect tweaks: parts may get fleshed out, and one minor destination will probably change.

## Early Middle: finding the Relic Hunters
1. **Ambush.** Elion and Soren track the [[Relic Hunters' Society|Relic Hunters]]. A peaceful approach fails, and a strike team (one officer, four members) ambushes them.
   - Elion drops the officer before he can destroy his entrance-credential seal and takes it.
   - When bluffed death threats fail, Elion threatens to break a young downed soldier's arm until the officer talks: the RHS is pulling Sensitives into survey work, promoting them fast (even a woman to officer), and routing anomaly reports to the **Maristadiz Survey Network**.
   - Soren swears he won't allow such methods again. Elion half-apologizes.
2. **The Ahafurt sanctuary.** In RHS robes, they slip into a small sanctuary and pull checkpoint data from a terminal.
   - [[Supporting Cast#Dioikitis & Michani|Dioikitis]] catches them: the team ID is on the *left* sleeve. His powered suit and plasma cannon outclass them both.
   - They escape by blasting a door open together with [[Forced Ki Assimilation]], and the sanctuary seals itself in stone.
   - Dioikitis fires a device into Elion's neck.
3. **Couriers.** They tail RHS couriers node to node for about four days to Maristadiz, finding the Survey Network HQ in a back-alley building. A long stakeout ("99 kegs of ale…") ends when a team with blue accents and a woman leader comes out.
4. **Evelyn.** She lures them to a dead end and binds both in chains.
   - About to kill them, she hesitates at Elion's furious outburst. Soren stops Elion's retaliatory punch, and she realizes they could have broken free at any time.
   - She is Evelyn, a Sensitive conscripted years ago who secretly opposes the RHS.
   - Nobody can pinpoint [[The Obelisk]]: the Ki around the capital saturates every Sensitive. But "one person" might. Her price is help altering a master regional terminal (MRT) in the Maristadiz sanctuary.

## Middle Middle: the Maristadiz sanctuary
5. **Server raid.** With Evelyn's forged credentials they reach the server wing.
   - Elion breaks in with Forced Ki Assimilation, and the neck device dumps a massive shock into him.
   - He holds the server room, using the racks as cover, while Evelyn scrubs every record of "Aeros."
6. **Dioikitis arrives** via the Teleportation Seal Matrix with his elite squad. He downloads a full copy of the local database before Evelyn's edit commits, then crushes them. Soren's skull is cracked.
7. **Soren awakens.** Pinned under Dioikitis's boot, Soren's revulsion triggers his awakening: purple [[Rune Eyes]], instant healing, overwhelming strength.
   - Dioikitis reacts with terror.
   - Soren absorbs Dioikitis's "Simple Mantra: Spearing Thunder" and fires it to collapse the sanctuary behind them, then his Ki gateways shut. (See [[Soren#Awakening (Book 1)]].)
8. **Aeros.** Evelyn hides them in her safehouse and reveals Aeros, her brother hidden under the floor and the one who can find the Obelisk. He says Soren especially "doesn't feel human."
   - New pact: find the Obelisk *and* deal with Dioikitis's database copy.
   - Elion's plan is to head for the capital and bank on Dioikitis following.
9. **The tracker and Michani.** Elion works out that the device is a tracker.
   - On the roof, he and Aeros bond over self-expectations: Aeros's father's "true man" ethos, and Elion's *"we're valued by what we are."*
   - Michani appears and introduces himself as newly recruited to Dioikitis's strike team. He probes about Soren's eyes ("Do you know what happens to peoples when they find a pair of those eyes among them?"), asks Elion to keep running, and leaves.
10. With almost a week burned and no word on Chara, they buy horses and a covered wagon and leave for the capital.

## End of Middle: the road to the capital
11. **The hunters regroup.** Dioikitis digs out, and Michani joins him. Michani knows the brothers are on a clock (the "true heir" sister) and proposes letting them run until they're cornered.
   - Dioikitis agrees but warns him not to indulge his sadism.
   - Killing Soren and keeping him secret now outranks even the Obelisk for Dioikitis.
12. **The tree and the flashback.** Humiliated and sleepless, Elion punches a tree, and the story flashes back to age 11: Soren's Forced Ki Assimilation, the demonstration, and Elion breaking his hand to match Soren's crater ("I love this…"). In the present, his arm is through the tree. (See [[Elion#Childhood]].)
13. **Training Aeros.** Elion declares he'll train Aeros. Evelyn is furious, and Elion tells Soren to "stay the fuck out of this." Aeros accepts.
   - On night rides Elion teaches Ki sensitivity (no jacket, in the cold) and then Forced Ki Assimilation, warning that overuse can cut Aeros off from Ki forever.
14. **Elion splits off.** Elion deteriorates, and the tracker shocks him when he tries to train. He slips away alone toward the capital to draw pursuit and scout; Soren calls him "selfish, like always."
   - Elion collapses on the road (a dream of Chara: "It's not your fault, Elion.") and realizes no one came for him, which is suspicious.
15. **The capital.** It's in lockdown: violent earthquakes, fleeing crowds, guards refusing entry.
   - Elion slips through a crack in the wall, ransacks abandoned homes for a seal, and pries one off a fleeing man's palm. It starts binding to his shoulder.
16. **Into the caves.** The others arrive, and Elion brings them in. Aeros feels a current of Ki and leads them through fissures, a shallow cave, and a forest to a cliff-face cave.
   - Evelyn wants to wait up top for the ambush. Elion refuses, and Soren overrules both: he'll hold off the hunters alone while the rest find the Obelisk.
   - Michani's plasma bolt hits Elion as Dioikitis's squad arrives. Elion carries Aeros and Evelyn into the caves.
