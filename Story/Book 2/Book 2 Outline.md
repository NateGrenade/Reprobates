# Book 2 Outline

Early working beats captured from brain dump. Middle connective tissue and refinements TBD.

## Setup & Book 1 Handoff
- [[Elion]] wakes after ~2 weeks, visits a healed [[Supporting Cast#Chara|Chara]], and has a tense reunion with [[Soren]].
- [[Supporting Cast#Evelyn & Aeros|Evelyn]] and [[Supporting Cast#Evelyn & Aeros|Aeros]] are granted sanctuary in Ahafurt under Soren's protection.
- [[Supporting Cast#Dioikitis & Michani|Dioikitis]]'s interrogation fails (killed by silencing seal); Michani is still at large.
- **Core mystery:** Nobody has ever tried to stop the [[Relic Hunters' Society|Relic Hunters' Society (RHS)]]. They covertly control Aurelian governments (including [[The Concord]]) and use world-wide [[Runes|runes]]/[[Seals|seals]] plus government-sponsored distractions to keep people complacent—letting them vent frustration without ever mustering the will to act—while hiding among them, unearthing relics, and kidnapping/sacrificing children to Anokpe.
- **Why the RHS panics & prepares to evacuate:** Soren having [[Rune Eyes]] publicly in Ahafurt is enough to make the RHS want to get off [[Aurelia]], since Rune Eyes foreshadow doom ([[Aioni]] annihilating worlds where Atheri are spotted). *(No Legion-ship detection.)*

## Act 1: The Split
- **Elion + Aeros:** Elion travels to the Concord capital to petition the King for a standing army to clear Ahafurt, taking Ki-blind Aeros with him (building their bond/chemistry while giving Soren and Evelyn room to develop).
  - *Note:* Considering moving Book 1's capital encounter to a minor city so Book 2 gets the full reveal of the capital (towering walls, floating district).
  - Elion is turned away by the Elite Concord Guard despite his noble status. He still has memory gaps from the Dioikitis fight and does not yet know he has Rune Eyes.
  - Needs a meaningful discovery in the capital (e.g., recognizing the capital's suppression symbols as matching the seal on his chest).
- **Soren + Evelyn in Ahafurt:** Exalted for his Rune Eyes, Soren decides to handle the RHS himself (early god complex). He and Evelyn run solo missions, grow closer (tacit feelings), and discover how deeply embedded the RHS is in Ahafurt.
  - Their father increasingly pressures Soren to back off, raising suspicion about their parents.

## Midpoint & Open Middle (~1/2 Through)
- **Aeros's return:** Aeros needs a reason to split back to Ahafurt after the first big chunk with Elion so he is back in danger alongside Chara by the climax (expanding Elion's stakes and duty beyond just his sister).
- **Capital convergence & non-RHS magic:** Elion and co. reach/converge on the capital by ~halfway through, leaving room for non-RHS obstacles and enemies—real Aurelian wizards using [[Seals]] and [[Mantras]] to a high degree—so the world feels larger than just RHS tech/mechanisms.
- *Open:* Specific middle beats connecting the Act 1 split, the capital investigation, and the Act 3 cascade.

## Act 3: Priests, Titans, & Climax
- **Priests & Titans:** As they poke deeper, they stumble onto [[Priests]] (rank XXVI+). In desperation, the RHS unleashes [[Titans]]—extremely powerful relic-beings kept in reserve for a doomsday scenario:
  1. First, to try to kill Soren and save face / contain the Rune Eyes leak.
  2. Second, when that fails, releasing many Titans to take everything and everyone they can before abandoning Aurelia. *(Sets up the Titans' larger existential role in Book 3.)*
- **Parents & Chest Seal Reveal:** Elion and Soren are adopted. Their parents placed the suppression seal on Elion's chest as a child to contain his volatile power (which is why his Rune Eyes only flickered in Book 1 when the Ki river partially fried it).
- **Elion vs. Michani (Church Fight):** Final confrontation inside a 1400s-style church. Michani toys with Elion as the church collapses around them and reveals his only motive is boredom. Elion's chest seal shatters in pure hate, triggering a massive outburst that nearly kills Michani before Michani escapes via a sanctuary teleporter.
- **Soren vs. Elion Standoff:** Soren arrives late and unilaterally declares they need to rally and stabilize/liberate the Concord cities, dismissing Elion again. Elion refuses to abandon Chara and Aeros in a compromised Ahafurt. Evelyn is torn between her brother and her feelings for Soren / sense of duty.
  - Ends in an explosive brother-vs-brother battle.
- **Book 3 Hook:** Elion and Soren are branded outward fugitives of [[The Concord]] just as the Concord begins to collapse under mounting pressure (leading into [[Concord Collapse Return]]).
