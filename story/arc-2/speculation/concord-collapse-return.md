# Return to the Concord During Its Collapse

**Status: SPECULATION**

During Arc 2, as the Aurelian Concord deteriorates under martial law, rising rebellions, and escalating Relic Hunters' Society raids, Elion and Soren are forced to return from their external endeavors.

Their return is motivated in part by the need to rescue their sister, Chara, who is caught within the increasingly dangerous conditions imposed across the Concord.

The exact circumstances of their separation from the Concord, the nature of their outside endeavors, the timing of the return, and the precise events surrounding Chara's rescue remain undecided.

This idea is intended to establish the broader Arc 2 situation as a genuine societal collapse rather than a localized conflict: Elion and Soren's personal objectives are eventually interrupted by the deterioration of the society they came from.
