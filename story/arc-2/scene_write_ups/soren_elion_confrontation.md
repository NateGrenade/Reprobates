# Soren / Elion Confrontation

**Status: SPECULATION**

> This is an exploratory scene write-up intended to prototype the Soren/Elion relationship, dialogue, escalation, and emotional dynamics during their confrontation in Arc 2.
>
> The prose and dialogue are not intended to represent the final written version of the scene. Most of the exact wording, narration, pacing, and presentation may be substantially revised or discarded. The primary value of this document is in preserving the underlying character interaction and dramatic beats.

---

Elion and Soren have been at odds for the past two arcs. And it had all come to a head now. Their forearms pressed against one another, and their ki flared and sparked between the two in a subtle back and forth as they stared each other down.

Since the beginning, they've been butting heads: Soren's more idealistic worldview has brought consistent judgement onto Elion, who acts with much less concern for others and much more for himself. Soren had always been the stronger of the two, even if barely, so Elion endured the constant criticism often accompanied by a jab or a smack on the top of the head meant to chastize. While some might see it as affectionate discipline from a brother to his younger sibling, Elion perceived it as a power play and a claiming of the moral high ground. Says who that Soren is better than him, just because he gives away more money or is obsessed with lofty ideals about reality that could hardly be attained?

This dynamic was one that always kept tension between the two. Elion did good things to receive the very rare affection and praise from his parents and peers. Yet he endured Soren moralizing to him as if desiring affirmation was somehow a grave evil. Soren's power plays and lecturing and berating only grew more intense after Soren manifested his Rune Eyes. It was weeks on end, but something shifted when Elion unlocked his own Rune Eyes during a brutal and tense battle.

Ever since then, Soren seemed... different. He was hesitant to hit Elion in the same demeaning way he had before, though no less sharp with his criticisms. In fact, Soren began scrutinizing Elion's actions more and more, despite Elion having softened some of his views in recent times. The two still engaged in regular sparring, as they always had, but there was a certain pressure that hadn't been present before. It seemed as if the both of them moved with more caution and were more reserved with the strength of their blows. Elion continued being bested in these matches, but it was clear the gap had closed... and the temptation to put more behind his blows grew every day for the young man, as Soren still held onto their supposed power differences.

That had been about a month ago, when they last sparred. Things in the Aurelian Concord had become massively chaotic as a group known as the Relic Hunters' Society began running wild out of nowhere. They had been previously known for being a clandestine group interested in relics for their historical value and to study. Now, they'd begun raiding every relic and magical item from towns, cities, and provinces alike. These raids often ended with bloodshed between them and the guards/local military, too. Order quickly began to fall as the government struggled in an all out guerilla war. Soren, Elion, as well as Kai and Amber - two of their companions - had set out to figure out what was going on and how to stop it. They'd discovered that the planet itself could be in danger, and that Elion/Soren were two people considered very valuable by the society, too, among many others. They had collectively tried to track down the other 'valuable' people for information and because of their strength, which would prove useful in quelling this threat. Soren constantly criticized Elion, became even more aggressive, and Elion responded in kind. The two were almost constantly at odds in their desires and decision making. Eventually, Elion elected to say that tracking down these other people was not proving a good use of their time: doing so would only slow and bog them down, and they needed to act regardless of whether the society kidnapped, killed, or did whatever to the remaining targets.

That's what caused the true argument. Elion practically told Soren that trying to save others, even those of the list that were young and defenseless, was a waste of time. A direct attack on Soren's ideals and beliefs.

> "What a mistake the universe made, giving you those eyes of yours. If I had them..."

> "There you go again, Soren. 'If only I had more power', is that right? You've gotten nothing BUT power recently, but you couldn't possibly stomach being second best, that's it, right? You hate having to respect the opinion of someone like me, knowing I'm not your... your inferior anymore?!"

> "You're winding yourself up over nothing, Elion, as per usual. Just shut the hell up and keep going: the next people on the list were last spotted just-"

> "You're not going to ignore me this time, Soren." Elion says in what amounts to a growl as he grabs Soren's shoulder forcefully

> "Every second you prove to the people around you why you don't deserve that power, Eli."

Elion then feels a familiar smack on the head, dismissing him like usual. That incessant smack which registers as mockery and nothing more, taunting him each time it's sting rears it's ugly head.

Soren only half turns before he sees Elion wind up a punch that, unlike previous sparring sessions, is holding nothing back. It takes all the speed he's got to twist his hips and body in time to collide his fist with Elion's at full force. Soren expected to overpower Elion at least by a little, but instead... total deadlock.

The ground beneath them both shudders before cracks spiderweb across the earth and rock below and around their feet. Soren flares his Ki, trying to add more power to his stalemated arm only to feel Elion's arm push back with just as much might. Elion takes a step foward, prompting Soren to do the same. Their arms bend so that their forearms are criss-crossed, and they can put their entire bodies behind the clash. Their Ki roars forth, sparking and zapping like miniature bolts of thunder between the two of them: Elion's a striking gold and Soren's a deep purple.

> "I'm not going to tell you again Elion. Stop acting like a child and being selfish. There's people to save"

> "You're so busy trying to save some random p-"

> "Shut the fuck up when I'm talking to you"

...

A beat goes by where neither speaks. Both of them are shocked at Soren's outburst, but Elion quickly shifts his expression from taken aback to a burning rage that's been simmering for far too many years.

> "Let's see if you talk that way after I break your jaw, Soren."
