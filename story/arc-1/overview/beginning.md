Arc 1 Highest Level Overview:

Start:

Elion and Soren are both already 21. They are twins, though unbeknownst to them, Soren is marginally older by some small amount.

The arc begins on a planet called Aurelia in a nation simply called "The Concord". The world operates on a similar technological level in most respects to a medieval European one. However, the key difference is the existence of small technological/magical artifacts (usually a mix of both: futuristic and technologically advanced). These artifacts are referred to as "relics"

Both Soren and Elion possess superior and superhuman levels of strength, a very abnormal ability for this world. Most people utilize relics or seals to exhibit abnormal powers - which themselves are typically external abilities or in effect.

Elion and Soren are nobles of this world whose parents are distant from them normally. Because of this, both of them have few real responsibilities as their parents handle noble duties

Elion is a cocky SOB who believes his status and power make him superior. He cares little for the poor - not actively mistreating them, but rather, simply having no regard for them. He sees their situations as their own problem and feels no duty towards them.

Elion receives conditional affection from his parents if his actions prop up their status. Thus, Elion (over time) formed a connection between self-aggrandization through flashy action, shows of power, etc. and being paid attention to by his parents.

Soren understands that his parents don't really care for HIM, and thus he doesn't play games to gain their favor like Elion. Seeing his lifestyle, Elion's actions, and the general state of the world, Soren became highly idealistic. He believes in doing what he can to help others, especially those who cannot defend themselves. He dislikes the system of nobility and monarchy, believing it neglects the average person greatly. This combined with his general disdain for his parents has created quite the animosity inside of Soren for them.

Soren tries to stick with Elion and tries to moderate/correct his behavior. Considering Soren is slightly stronger, he's always been able to act as an "older brother" despite them not knowing which if either is actually older in actuality. Soren hopes he can influence Elion to be better, knowing they could have some influence to change the system. That said, his chastising can breach into belittlement and total disregard of his opinion/statements. Long story short, Soren deep down sees himself as superior to Elion, in a somewhat ironic way.

The above has also bred some rancor in Elion towards Soren. This makes their relationship complex by the time the story picks up. They have good chemistry, talk, laugh together, etc. But there are moments when they are at odds, annoyed with one another for different reasons, or disagree heavily.

Elion chooses to hunt relics - a flashy job for a flashy guy. He competes directly with a more clandestine group called the Relic Hunters, seeing their success as a challenge to his status and a way to prop himself up. Soren, of course, accompanies him for the prior reasons and knows that, going around adventuring like Elion, he'll have more opportunities to help people out. He also prefers to not live at home and indulge in his wealth or be forced to see his parents, which is arguably a larger contribution to his reasoning to stick with Elion over being home.

Soren and Elion have a much younger sister (about 11ish years old) named Chara. She was born a sickly child, though she herself is upbeat, adventurous and optimistic. Her sickly nature didn't go away as she aged, and she struggles with basic activities (winded after exertion, difficulty getting around easy, etc.), has a weak constitution, and is very susceptible to sickness.

Chara takes after Elion and Soren in a unique way. Her unwavering confidence as well as her desire to go out into the world and explore is learned from Elion, but she lacks Elion's disregard for others and his performative nature. She also has learned some ideals from Soren (though her depth of knowledge isn't complex considering her young age) but she lacks Soren's pride, pessimism, and subtle savior complex.

Despite being strong, neither Elion nor Soren know they are Atheri or non-human. Neither have their rune eyes, either. They also are aware of the magic system (Ki, Seals, Mantras, etc.) and know they need to Awaken/Be Called in order to become stronger and gain access to mantras, but the process of Being Called is somewhat mysterious to them as most people who go through it find it difficult to explain. Thus, both of them want to achieve that power - for their personal gain and also because of their competitive nature between one another.

Chara exposes both Elion and Soren's flaws simply by existing, not in a condescending way, but through innocence and good intentions. Ironically, Elion's own philosophy of not caring for those weaker than him, and believing they deserve their fate, is completely refuted by his care, love, and defensiveness of Chara. Chara is perhaps the weakest person in the series, and yet Elion is a classic protective older brother to her, especially given the age difference which only exacerbates this gap. With semi-absentee parents, Elion and Soren both act like father-like figures to her while maintaining some of that brotherly aspect. To reiterate and sum up: Chara exposes the problems of Soren's ideology by revealing, through contrast, his personal pride, pessimism, etc. However, Chara reveals Elion's flaws because of how Elion treats her, and not through anything of her own doing.
