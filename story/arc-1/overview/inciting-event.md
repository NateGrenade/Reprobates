Challenge to Status Quo:

Chara's health has been declining despite top-tier physicians working on her. The decline started becoming more rapid after Elion decided to take Chara with him on an adventure to find a relic, satisfying her long-standing desire to do so. During the adventure, Chara saw a man being mugged in an alleyway. Despite Elion ignoring it and trying to walk past, she broke free of Elion's grip and ran back to help. Being a frail young girl, her attempt was in vain. The man doing the mugging saw the girl and backhanded her, severely injuring her. Elion showed back up in horror, and the viewer is not shown what Elion does to the man in that flashback.

This all happened around one year ago, and since then, Elion has been searching for ways to help Chara. Her physical injuries healed, but the incident left her drained and increasingly weak over time, making Elion more and more desperate to help.

This led Elion on a journey through books and libraries to find answers, of which he came across two. A so-called "Aethercrux" in old legends was one possible answer, which would allow Elion to make her well and then some. Elion concluded that such a thing could never really exist. The other possible solution he'd come across was another legendary but more well-known relic, "The Obelisk," which is a relic of mysterious power constructed to heal ailments of all kinds. It was buried centuries ago, as far as Elion could find in records and myth, but he never gave up looking for clues about it.

When Chara falls severely ill, physicians tell Soren and Elion that she perhaps has a few weeks left at her current rate. This prompts Elion to do something he'd refused to do until now: ask the Relic Hunters for help.

This forces Elion to, first of all, find the Relic Hunters. Elion's initial instinct is to get information out of them by force, but Soren reminds him that he shouldn't be rash, and elects to accompany Elion for the sake of Chara.

Thus, their journey begins to track down and uncover the Relic Hunters. They need to get information out of them about the Obelisk, which Elion, despite the mainstream consensus of its mythological nature, has become convinced exists.

The dynamic from the onset is highly tense. Elion and Soren are on a strict time limit, and worried for their sister. Elion is less rational than usual, leading to more correction from Soren and more tension between their decisions, too.

Of course, side characters have not yet been fully established, so as the arc goes on, it is imperative that people join up with Elion and Soren to flesh out the cast.
