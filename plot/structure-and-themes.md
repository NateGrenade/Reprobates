**Central Theme: The Cross vs. The Throne**
This is the thematic label for the story's central conflict. Soren seeks the Throne: mechanical, calculated top-down control meant to eliminate suffering, which instead breeds tyranny. Elion embraces the Cross: stepping into the mud, absorbing the world's brokenness, and enduring others' suffering unilaterally.

**"The Four Men"**
An early personal organizing concept numbering the main cast by thematic/chronological order: Aioni (1st), Egoei (2nd), Soren (3rd), Elion (4th).

**Very Most Basic Structure**
- Arc 1: The Relic Hunters' Society (led by Dioikitis and Michani) serve as the antagonist faction; Aeros's suffering triggers Elion's Being Called.
- Arc 2: Aurelia, Elion's home planet, is destroyed, expanding the scope of the setting.
- Arcs 3–5: Elion travels with his Companions/friends (an Aurelian survivor and a Galactic companion) and faces the Indifferentist Bounty Hunter and Legion, both serving Aioni. The Companions abandon Elion in Arc 5 when his morality darkens under Egoei's influence.
- End of Arc 5: Elion's rage-fueled assault on Aioni's capital destroys an entire city and ends with Aioni near death. Egoei then personally kills Aioni to obtain what he needs (locating the bio-engineering lab and the Aethercrux) and kills himself on the battlefield to be resurrected — without explaining any of this to Elion. Aioni's imposed peace collapses into faction warfare immediately after.
- Arc 6: A galactic civil war, incited by Elion's own mistakes, which he ultimately fails to stop by the series' end.

*Note the above is VERY simplified. For details just ask questions or, if available, refer to dedicated arc files.

**Ending**
Elion ends the main series at a material, physical, and relational low — having lost nearly everything, including relationships with old companions who reject him — while still holding to his convictions and continuing to serve, expecting nothing in return. His final action in the series is thinking to himself "I need to end this war" as he walks off towards his ship. No explicit divine reward or sign of cosmic vindication is given (see power_system/being_called.md for the reasoning behind that choice).
