# Reprobates workshop

Load `reprobates-lore` for vault access and conventions. The vault is canon. Drive holds Nathan's freewrite docs (the Reprobates "Story" folders, e.g. the Arc 1 "v1" doc); read those in full, never from snippets. Everything here is read-only until Nathan asks to save; then write via `reprobates-lore`.

## Continuity check
Read the whole scene first. List its checkable claims: character traits, ages, and relationships; how powers are used and what they cost; timeline position; who knows what. Read the 1–6 relevant notes, then report:
- **Contradicts:** quote the scene line and the note line, and name the note.
- **Shaky ground:** canon is speculation-only, silent, or ambiguous on the point.
- **Consistent:** one line covering the load-bearing points.

New material that canon doesn't cover is a gap, not a contradiction; flag it as worth formalizing. No prose or style critique here.

## Plot ideas and scene beats
Start from Nathan's premise and retrieve the relevant character, world, and power-system notes. When requested, brainstorm plot alternatives and outline scene beats as concise planning notes: actions, choices, consequences, tension, and information revealed. Nathan creates all prose and direct writing for the books. Do not generate dialogue, internal monologue, descriptive narrative, scene prose, replacement passages, or manuscript text, even as a rough example. Do not use existing scenes as a writing-style template.

Mark assistant-generated ideas as proposals or speculation and distinguish them from Nathan's supplied ideas and established canon. Flag possible canon conflicts; do not silently resolve them or establish new facts. Track accepted/rejected ideas and history only from Nathan's actual decisions. Do not save anything unless Nathan asks; use the existing vault conventions and an appropriate existing location instead of assuming an Arc N path.

## Canon sync (Drive → vault)
Go through the Drive docs most recent first. Report per section:
- **Migrated:** which note covers it.
- **Not yet migrated:** a candidate for drafting.
- **Conflict:** Drive says X, the vault says Y. The vault wins unless Nathan says otherwise.

End with a one-line count. If Drive isn't connected, say so and offer a vault-only pass.

## Version diff
Compare the two full texts. Lead with substantive changes grouped by topic (added, removed, altered), then add one line for phrasing-only polish. When diffing Drive against the vault, any divergence means Drive is stale on that point. Don't assume the higher version number is the keeper; report neutrally. "Essentially identical" is a valid answer.
