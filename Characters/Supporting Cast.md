## Chara
[[Elion]] and [[Soren]]'s frail, sickly 11-year-old sister, and a moral mirror to both brothers.
- Born sickly but upbeat. Publicly touted as the "true heir" to the Ahafurt nobility (see [[Book 1 Plot Holes and Fixes]] §5).
- Her decline is triggered by Elion ignoring a mugger she tried to stop. The mugger's backhand left a fist-sized bruise across her jaw, and the strain on her already weakened immune system left her sick and worsening ever since. This fuels Elion's search for [[The Obelisk]].
- By the end of Book 1 she is healed: unmarked, running around outside, and fussed over by the public. (How the healing happened isn't in the draft yet.)
- She dies in Arc 3 when [[Aurelia]] is destroyed. The one thing Elion keeps afterward is her parchment drawing of the three of them as a stick-figure family.
- Her death (and the desire to resurrect her) is later tied to the temptation [[Egoei]] and the [[Aethercrux]] represent for Elion.

## Evelyn & Aeros
Mortal companions in Book 1, and siblings. Both are [[Innate Techniques|Sensitives]].

**Evelyn**
- An officer in the [[Relic Hunters' Society]], forcibly conscripted roughly 8 years before Book 1 (tentative). She was only recently promoted to officer and leads a blue-accented survey team.
- She fights with a mid-range simple seal that conjures Ki-enhanced chains. She's adequate at mid range, not primarily a warrior.
- Against the RHS from the start, with forged credentials and inside contacts. She hid Aeros for years and staged his disappearance (see [[Book 1 Plot Holes and Fixes]] §1).
- In Book 1 she trades her help finding the Obelisk for the brothers' help purging Aeros from a master regional terminal, which makes her a fugitive.
- She distrusts Elion, especially after he trains Aeros and abandons them at the Obelisk, but finally lets him take Aeros to the healing circle.

**Aeros**
- 15. Raised largely alone and hidden (once under the floor of Evelyn's Maristadiz safehouse).
- The most gifted Sensitive the RHS has tested. He feels Ki as warmth and is the one who finds [[The Obelisk]].
- Guilty, driven, and self-taught from picture books. His late father taught him that a man's fight is "yours and yours alone." He and Elion bond over self-expectations.
- Elion trains him in Ki sensitivity and [[Forced Ki Assimilation]].
- At the Obelisk he uses FKA to shove Evelyn out of Michani's strike, shattering his leg, and takes Michani's blade through his neck. Near-dead (not dead), he is healed by the Obelisk when Elion throws him into the rune circle.
- He wakes unable to feel Ki at all.

**Planned (not yet in draft):** Aeros's loss of Ki sensitivity is temporary, but it takes him all of Book 2 and the better part of Book 3 to get it back.

## Dioikitis & Michani
Book 1's [[Relic Hunters' Society]] antagonists. They are *not* the RHS's leaders; they barely scratch the surface of how deep the organization goes.

**Dioikitis**
- A famous, high-ranking RHS commander stationed at the Ahafurt sanctuary, leading an elite four-person strike squad. He answers to higher-ups and a bloated bureaucracy.
- Arrogant, mirroring and surpassing Elion's Book 1 disposition.
- Devout: religiously bound to relics, which belong hidden away for private worship, veneration, and experimentation. He swears "By Anokpe." He is also tactically pragmatic.
- Wears an elite powered suit (enhanced strength and speed, thrusters, IR vision, overdrive, a plasma hand cannon, an electrified techno-staff). His mantra is "Simple Mantra: Spearing Thunder."
- Terrified of [[Rune Eyes]]. Once Soren's eyes appear, killing Soren and every witness, and keeping Soren secret even from the RHS, outranks the Obelisk itself.

**Michani**
- A half-mechanical cyborg ("a metal abomination pretending to be human") recruited onto Dioikitis's squad.
- Doesn't need air, and reboots after his systems are scrambled. Flies by jet. Fights with plasma cannons, a seal-fed flamethrower, and a retractable blade.
- Long-lived ("when you've lived as long as I have"; "I haven't had this kind of fun in decades"), with intelligence far beyond his rank.
- A deeply sadistic hunter who lives for "the chase" and treats people as "the ultimate game of chess." He nearly kills Aeros and mocks Elion as his mirror ("two sides of a coin").
- Insubordinate. He refuses to help Dioikitis once Elion's eyes appear, and flies off.
- **Allegiance undecided:** he possibly reaches the RHS's deeper layer, but there's no settled link to Egoei.

**Planned (not yet in draft): Dioikitis's fate**
- Dioikitis survives Book 1 and is imprisoned by Soren for his crimes, something unheard of for any RHS member, let alone an officer. His gear is confiscated and destroyed, including his database copy.
- Elion interrogates him. Dioikitis refuses, then breaks down in fear, urging Elion to kill himself, Soren, Chara, their entire bloodline if they must: "You need to purge your diseased blood, I'm begging!"
- When fear finally drives him to reveal the truth, a silencing seal appears on his tongue, spreads across his face, and kills him instantly by crushing his skull.

## The Companions
In Arcs 4–6, Elion travels with an Aurelian survivor and a Galactic companion (either an Imperial defector or a stunted Atheri clone). They abandon Elion in Arc 6 when his morality darkens under Egoei's influence, and refuse to fully welcome him back by the story's end.

## The Indifferentist Bounty Hunter
Appears in Arcs 4–5. Has died thousands of times via his own power and believes the universe is cold, caring only about survival in the moment. His ability is a form of quantum/temporal cloning: he splits the timeline for 1–5 seconds into clones and solidifies whichever branch does the most damage, but experiences the deaths of all the failed timelines — an experience that broke his mind into apathetic nihilism. Elion defeats him by sapping his own [[Lattice|Ki]], letting himself be impaled, and using hyper-regeneration to crush/sever the hunter's arm — then stopping himself from finishing the hunter off in a rage to go save locals instead, shattering the hunter's indifferent worldview.

## Legion
An insane enforcer serving [[Aioni]] who delights in destroying planets and inflicting pain. Aioni sends him after the twins once he can locate them; he arrives at the end of Book 3 (see [[Structure and Themes#Trilogy One and the Aioni thread]]). Elion eventually kills him, but the kill leaves Elion empty rather than satisfied.
