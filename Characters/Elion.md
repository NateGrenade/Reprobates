Elion is the protagonist of Reprobates, an engineered member of the Atheri, a powerful and long living race. He was created as a fetus by [[Egoei]], who functions as his (and [[Soren]]'s) father/creator rather than a biological parent — making Elion and Soren brothers in that engineered sense.

## Childhood
- As a boy, Elion was torn between martial arts and what his parents wanted him to be. In truth he enjoyed learning and scholarship and wanted to go into diplomacy and politics, which won no praise from his parents or peers. He studied in his free time and trained as told, earning respect as "gifted."
- At 11, Soren taught himself [[Forced Ki Assimilation]] and cratered a stone wall at the family's monthly public demonstration, becoming the "prodigy." Elion couldn't, and his parents' attention and the public's awe drained away.
- Elion stole textbooks and trained in secret for weeks, bloodying his fists on a cliff face. At the next demonstration he insisted on striking the wall, matched (or slightly beat) Soren's crater, and broke his hand doing it. His father nodded in approval, nobody noticed the broken hand, and Elion thought: *"I love this…"*
- He still uses a candle to time training holds and keeps lengthening it. Chara once called him weird but said his trying "really really hard" was awesome; that memory returns at his awakening.

## Rune Eyes
Elion carries the [[Rune Eyes]], an ability originally manifested by an ancestor named Formidos in a desperate, failed attempt to cure death. Rune Eyes fulfill the user's deepest desire through raw power, but imperfectly — Formidos gained immense lifespan, not true immortality. In general, they amplify whatever self-focused desire drives the user.

Visually, as a user's ideological conviction hardens, glowing cracks propagate from the sclera down the neck and body, eventually meeting at the heart — the "point of no return." Overuse past the safe limit locks the eye circuit: the eyes revert as his Ki gateway shuts, causing total spiritual blockage and temporary loss of lineage traits like regeneration and longevity.

Elion's eyes are gold. With them he can see [[Lattice|the Lattice]] and people's connections to it.

**Half-awakening (Book 1):** Elion only half-awakens in Book 1, in a Ki river beneath [[The Obelisk]] after Dioikitis blasts a hole through his heart.
- It's driven by internal conflict: love for [[Supporting Cast#Chara|Chara]] (the memory of her praising his "trying") against his self-focused striving (*"I can't let myself be weak… Since when does trying matter"*; *"I have to win… I have to reclaim my glory. I have to save Chara."*).
- Because it's only half, the eyes flicker and can't hold a stable form, and the sclera crack and re-seal.
- His full awakening comes late in Book 2, when he also breaks the suppression seal inhibiting his Ki (see [[Structure and Themes#Trilogy One and the Aioni thread]]).

**White-gold aura:** his power manifests as a white-gold substance between flowing mist and fire that cascades over his body and burns his skin. When he pushes adaptation too far, it goes haywire: the burns spread, and his skin chars black and flakes away. This charring is specific to Elion.

Elion pushes his Rune Eyes into a rage/hate-fueled overdrive state at multiple points. In his first fight with Soren in late arc 3, he retains partial lucidity even in that state (shown by parrying Soren's final attack). Later, fighting [[Aioni]], who's taken everything from him, he fully loses himself in that state, killing thousands of innocents in the process — this is foreshadowed by the Soren fight and is meant to show how his selfish hate ultimately drives his most destructive actions.

## Adaptation Power
**Control is over degree, not function.**
- Elion controls when adaptation begins and how hard it runs. He doesn't micromanage how it works: specific adaptations are driven by instinct, but he could cancel them if he chose. (It's like controlling how fast a car goes without generating the engine's force or physically moving the car.)
- That control only arrives with his full awakening (late Book 2), and how it works will be elaborated in Book 2. During his Book 1 half-awakening, he has no complete, conscious control over when adaptation activates.

**In Book 1** it shows up as adaptation, not separate powers:
- He learns Dioikitis's movements and attack patterns until he's acting before the strikes land. Dioikitis reads it as prediction.
- He instinctively structures miniature Ki lattices across and under his skin that make Dioikitis's electricity all but impotent.

**Costs:**
- The power amplifies his own hate when used with hate.
- Its primary cost isn't a hard limit but intense agony during use and lasting pain afterward that never fades. Each meaningful adaptation leaves a small amount of permanent, compounding pain, mirroring a small amount of permanent, compounding strength growth.
- In-fight power growth itself is not permanent: his adapted body and abilities are sustained only by active output during combat and regress once the fight ends.

(See [[Adaptation Framework]] for the general power-tier framework this is built on.)

## Tools and techniques (Book 1)
- **[[Forced Ki Assimilation]]**, his signature burst technique since childhood.
- **Seals:** he looks down on them (*"seals are so cheap. A real fighter doesn't need outside tools like that"*). Out of options in the capital, he pries a water seal off a fleeing man's palm and binds it to his shoulder. His awakening accelerates the binding, and he overfills it to cut Dioikitis with a razor-thin water jet.
- **The RHS tracker:** Dioikitis fires a tracking device into his neck. It shocks him whenever he draws Ki and lets the [[Relic Hunters' Society]] follow him. He turns it against Michani by deliberately overloading it, and it detonates in the Ki river.

## The Zenith
Elion's Zenith, or the soul-weapon manifested once his Rune Eyes fully mature, is a golden, flaming sword that cuts through anything and everything physical and leaves permanent wounds on Atheri (who can typically regenerate). He retains this and only this power even after his Rune Eyes burn out, acting as a symbol of his honor in some sense (though the sword is not as strong as it is with full Rune Eyes boost, of course)

## Ending
In the final confrontation against Soren, Elion wins by destroying the [[Aethercrux]] rather than using it, sacrificing his eyes/divinity/power to do so. He does this instead of using it to fulfill his own desires (such as resurrecting Chara, fixing his past mistakes/the war he caused after killing Aioni, defeating Soren in the moment).
