**Origin: how Elion and Soren came to Aurelia**

*Provisional — broad strokes locked in, exact mechanics/timeline to be refined.*

This is the personal, Aurelia-specific thread of Egoei's broader plan (see characters/Egoei/overview.md) to scatter roughly 1,000 engineered Atheri fetuses across the galaxy — both to distract Aioni's search and to keep the fetuses safer than concentrating them in one place.

Elion and Soren's fetuses are among those scattered, arriving together on Aurelia and growing into infants there (exact circumstances of the landing/early infancy still to be nailed down).

A noble couple on Aurelia, facing severe fertility problems and needing successors to preserve their line, sought out adoptive children. They chose Elion and Soren specifically for two reasons: the boys were already an inseparable pair, satisfying the couple's wish for an established sibling unit rather than assembling one from strangers, and even as infants the two displayed abnormal physical strength and Ki reserves that caught the couple's attention.

The couple raised Elion and Soren disguised as their own biological children, preserving the family's bloodline legitimacy and public image. Neither Elion nor Soren learn the truth of their origin during Arc 1 — consistent with them not knowing they're Atheri, or that they lack Rune Eyes, at the series' start.
