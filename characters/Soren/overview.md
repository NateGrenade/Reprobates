Soren is Elion's brother and rival, engineered as a fetus by Morpheus alongside Elion — they're brothers in that engineered sense, not through shared biological parents.

**Ideology**
Soren's worldview is shaped mainly by witnessing widespread suffering and how power gets abused between people — personal loss (Chara's death, Aurelia's fall) is the inciting event, not the core cause. He becomes a "post-Marxist" figure who believes suffering only ends by dissolving all distinctions and disparities between people: cultures, religions, belief systems, and so on. His first concrete move toward this is severing all beings from the Lattice so nobody but himself retains access to Ki, reasoning that Ki/power is the single biggest source of disparity between people — he intends to address other social and cultural distinctions afterward.

Despite this ideological framing, Soren is read as ultimately sharing Morpheus's core selfishness: he believes ending his own suffering (including suffering caused by others' suffering) is worth any cost. This is part of the "Cross vs. Throne" contrast at the heart of the story — Soren seeks the Throne (mechanical, calculated top-down control meant to eliminate suffering, which breeds tyranny instead), while Elion embraces the Cross (stepping into the mud and enduring others' suffering unilaterally).

**Relationship with Elion**
Early in development, Soren wasn't Elion's brother at all — he was just a character killed during Aurelia's fall. He was developed into Elion's brother/mirror figure later. Despite being rivals, Elion and Soren are forced to team up to defeat Morpheus. One planned structural change (in progress) has them fighting alongside each other watched by Morpheus, or skipping the team-up fight sequence entirely.

An earlier, formative conflict between them: a sparring session-turned-real fight, triggered by an argument over whether to keep tracking kidnap targets of the Relic Hunters' Society. Both activate their Rune Eyes; Elion pushes his past the safe upper limit.

**Death**
In the final clash, Soren refuses reconciliation with Elion and dies trying to draw more power from the Aethercrux — his soul is consumed by it in the attempt.
