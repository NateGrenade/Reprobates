Elion is the protagonist of Reprobates, an engineered member of the Atheri, a powerful and long living race. He was created as a fetus by Morpheus, who functions as his (and Soren's) father/creator rather than a biological parent — making Elion and Soren are brothers in that engineered sense.

**Rune Eyes**
Elion carries the Rune Eyes, an ability originally manifested by an ancestor named Formidos in a desperate, failed attempt to cure death. Rune Eyes fulfill the user's deepest desire through raw power, but imperfectly — Formidos gained immense lifespan, not true immortality. In general, they amplify whatever self-focused desire drives the user.

Visually, as a user's ideological conviction hardens, glowing cracks propagate from the sclera down the neck and body, eventually meeting at the heart — the "point of no return." Overuse past the safe limit causes the eye circuit to lock up and turn charcoal-colored, causing total spiritual blockage and temporary loss of lineage traits like regeneration and longevity.

Elion pushes his Rune Eyes into a rage/hate-fueled overdrive state at multiple points. In his first fight with Soren in late arc 2, he retains partial lucidity even in that state (shown by parrying Soren's final attack). Later, fighting Aioni, who's taken everything from him, he fully loses himself in that state, killing thousands of innocents in the process — this is foreshadowed by the Soren fight and is meant to show how his selfish hate ultimately drives his most destructive actions.

**Adaptation Power**
Elion's adaptation ability is fully self-controlled from day one — "losing control" is something he consciously allows himself, not something that happens to him. The power amplifies his own hate when used with hate. Its primary cost isn't a hard limit but intense agony during use and lasting pain afterward that never fades — each meaningful adaptation leaves a small amount of permanent, compounding pain, mirroring a small amount of permanent, compounding strength growth. In-fight power growth itself is not permanent; his adapted body/abilities are sustained only by active output during combat and regress once the fight ends. (See power_system/adaptation-framework.md for the general power-tier framework this is built on.)

**The Zenith**
Elion's Zenith, or the soul-weapon manifested once his Rune Eyes fully mature, is a golen, flaming sword that cuts through anything and everything physical and leaves permanent wounds on Atheri (who can typically regenerate). He retains this and only this power even after his Rune Eyes burn out, acting as a symbol of his honor in some sense (though the sword is not as strong as it is with full Rune Eyes boost, of course)

**Ending**
In the final confrontation against Soren, Elion wins by destroying the Aethercrux rather than using it, sacrificing his eyes/divinity/power to do so. He does this instead of using it to fulfill his own desires (such as resurrecting Chara, fixing his past mistakes/the war he caused after killing Aioni, defeating Soren in the moment).
