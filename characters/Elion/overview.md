Elion is the protagonist of Reprobates, an engineered member of the Atheri, an immortal race. He was created as a fetus by Morpheus, who functions as his (and Soren's) father/creator rather than a biological parent — Elion and Soren are brothers in that engineered sense.

**Rune Eyes**
Elion carries the Rune Eyes, an ability originally manifested by an ancestor named Formidos in a desperate, failed attempt to cure death. Rune Eyes fulfill the user's deepest desire through raw power, but imperfectly — Formidos gained immense lifespan, not true immortality. In general, they amplify whatever self-focused desire drives the user.

Visually, as a user's ideological conviction hardens, glowing cracks propagate from the sclera down the neck and body, eventually meeting at the heart — the "point of no return." Overuse past the safe limit causes the eye circuit to lock up and turn charcoal-colored, causing total spiritual blockage and temporary loss of lineage traits like regeneration and longevity.

Elion pushes his Rune Eyes into a rage/hate-fueled overdrive state at multiple points. In the earlier Soren fight he retains partial lucidity even in that state (shown by parrying Soren's final attack). Later, fighting an opponent who's taken everything from him, he fully loses himself in that state, killing thousands of innocents in the process — this is foreshadowed by the Soren fight and is meant to show how his selfish hate ultimately drives his most destructive actions.

**Adaptation Power**
Elion's adaptation ability is fully self-controlled from day one — "losing control" is something he consciously allows himself, not something that happens to him. The power amplifies his own hate when used with hate. Its primary cost isn't a hard limit but intense agony during use and lasting pain afterward that never fades — each meaningful adaptation leaves a small amount of permanent, compounding pain, mirroring a small amount of permanent, compounding strength growth. In-fight power growth itself is not permanent; his adapted body/abilities are sustained only by active output during combat and regress once the fight ends. (See power_system/adaptation-framework.md for the general power-tier framework this is built on.)

**The Zenith**
Elion's ultimate weapon is The Zenith (Sword of Grace), a soul-sword that cuts through absolute rules and leaves permanent wounds on immortals. Manifesting it requires permanently burning out both his Rune Eyes and his right arm.

**Arc and Ending**
Chara, Elion's frail, sickly younger sister, dies after a decline triggered by Elion ignoring a mugger she tried to stop — this fuels his search for the Obelisk relic. In the climactic confrontation with Revenant, Elion's rage boils over and destroys an entire city; after his own powers burn out he keeps beating a near-death Revenant with his bare hands. Morpheus then personally kills Revenant to get what he needs and kills himself on the battlefield to be resurrected, without explaining any of it to Elion — deepening Elion's isolation. In the final confrontation, Elion wins by destroying the Aethercrux rather than using it, sacrificing his eyes/divinity/power to do so, rather than using it to fulfill his own desires (such as resurrecting Chara).

Nathan has deliberately decided against giving Elion an explicit "total Ki mastery" or any divine-signified reward at the end of the series — drawing on Job, the early Christians, and Mother Teresa's decades-long lack of felt divine presence, so the story's theme of total self-sacrifice isn't undercut by an implied cosmic vindication. Elion is intended to end the main series at a material, physical, and relational low, having lost nearly everything — including relationships with old companions who reject him — while still holding to his convictions and continuing to serve, expecting nothing in return.
