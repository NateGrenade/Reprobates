Egoei is a philosophically articulate antagonist and, in an engineered sense, the father/creator of both Elion and Soren.

**Backstory/relation to Aioni**
Egoei and Aioni were once best friends about a million years before the beginning of the story. The two were prodigies even among the Atheri, respected and seen as the crown jewls of their generation. Despite their young age at the time, they worked together and successfully created the Aethercrux. It was a device unparalleled by any prior to it, magnifying the Atheri's power even further. It had the unique ability, among many others, to grant eternal life to whomever the user pleased. That said, all the power of the Aethercrux was generated through the sacrifice of other mortal beings, making the device itself inherently unethical and destructive. Egoei was hesitant about the sacrifices required for the Aethercrux, but excited with his success at first. Egoei's idealistic nature clashed with typical Atheri desires, but it also clashed with the nature of the Aethercrux, making Egoei reluctant about using it despite how magnificently useful and powerful it was. As thousands of years passed, Egoei saw that the now-immortal Atheri became bored with their own lives. A sort of deep existential dread settled on the people over time, breeding ever more cruel ways to stay "entertained" and to fill their time. The Atheri had already not been too concerned about so-called "lesser" people, but eventually, they began torturing, enslaving, and "playing" with their lives as a source of vain laughter and enjoyment.

Egoei and Aioni were both horrified. Egoei considered using the Aethercrux to stop them, but couldn't bear to kill even more people to utilize its power, instead opting to persuade and stop them himself as he could. Aioni was less reserved in his approach. He decided that eternal life and infinite power bred detached monsters, and he decided that the Atheri as a whole were a perpetual force of evil - disrupting peace, bringing death, and ontologically incapable of good or change. Aioni used the Aethercrux's power, fueled by billions of souls he deemed necessary sacrifices, and personally murdered every living Atheri save for a small few, who escaped to the edges of the cosmos. Egoei tried to stop and persuade Aioni to give his people another chance.

Aioni personally left Egoei blinded and mortally wounded during that altercation, and Egoei barely survived and escaped.

From then on, Aioni decided to personally hunt down any remaining Atheri, including Egoei. But during this time, he saw that death, war, and cruelty didn't fade in the galaxy even with the Atheri dead. His philosophy of keeping the peace through power and brutal measures developed further, and Aioni eventually established a ruling empire that grew over countless millennia until it spanned a massive portion of the galaxy, which is the point at which we begin the story of Reprobates.

After escaping into the far reaches of the galaxy, Egoei lived out thousands of lifetimes under the constant siege of Aioni. He tried to bond with people, but he was often outcast for his nature and their collective memory/fear of the Atheri. Egoei tried to uphold his ideals and desire to do good, but was continually spit upon by the world and attacked by Aioni. Egoei saw a galaxy of people filled with selfishness and hate who couldn't look beyond themselves at/for anyone else. They killed and enslaved each other, and reviled him for merely existing. 

That said, Egoei eventually fell deeply in love and started a family on a distant planet, finding a rare bastion of people unaffected by war and unaware of his nature. However, Aioni's bounty hunters eventually found them. They branded his family as traitors while trying to kill Egoei, and despite begging them to spare his family and take him instead, they murdered every last person he loved. Those very bounty hunters were other people, other mortals. They were trained by Aioni, but their job was only to kill him, not his family. This was what cemented his shift toward believing mortal compassion is worthless and mortal lives don't merit consideration. His descent into total egoism was finalized: his ideals had profited him nothing. It was clear the universe didn't care for him, and thus he shouldn't care for it in return. The only thing that matters to him now is his own self: his pleasure, and the removal of his pain.

**Ideology**
Egoei's rhetoric draws on ethical egoism and immortal-scale consequentialism. He lives in hiding, spreading his philosophy onto Elion as a chosen successor. He's ironically "selfless" only in the narrow sense that he values his people's continued existence even above himself. That said, he only values the repopulation/resurrection of his species so highly because the loss of his people was the greatest catalyst of his own pain, making its reversal - in another sense - the greatest form of pleasure and personal joy.

**Plan and Death**
Egoei's goal was to create a new race of people who thought like him in a universe devoid of any people to oppose or hurt them. It was with people like Aioni plaguing their old race that caused their death. Thus, after spending thousands and thousands of years reverse engineering his DNA and the DNA of the cadavers of old Atheri, he perfected the ability to create and grow new Atheri. These "New Atheri" were made to surpass even his own strength one day. That said, being constantly under threat of death from Aioni and his hunters, and being severely/permanently weakened after the fight with Aioni, he knew he wouldn't have the time to create too many, let alone raise them all in one place. Thus, he made as many as he could before being found and scattered the fetuses across the galaxy, on whom Egoei placed specific seals that fed off their Ki, allowing him to track them if close enough. He just needed one Atheri to survive and grow strong enough that the seal would be trackable even across the galaxy for Egoei, letting him track the new Atheri down and indoctrinate him. 

Indoctrinating at least one Atheri was crucial becuase the initial batch of new Atheri weren't made to repopulate per se, but instead, they were designed to distract Aioni as he was forced to track them down and kill them out of fear. Egoei's bet and plan was that the strongest of those fetuses, including Soren and Elion, wouldn't be found (since he scattered them to the furthest reaches of the galaxy, away from the distraction-cluster of New Atheri he'd made to fool/occupy Aioni). The strongest of the New Atheri were made with one purpose in mind: to kill Aioni, since Egoei was now too weak to even try. This plan works when Elion does exactly this under Egoei's tutelade. 

He uses Aioni's dead body to track the location of the Aethercrux - mixing his and Aioni's ki to remotely connect to it. He also forces Aioni to divulge the location of his now-hidden cloning chamber - which was repossessed by Aioni but unable to be destroyed due to a hyper-complex seal Egoei placed on it. Once he obtains this info, and remotely connects to the cloning chamber, he kills himself and transfers his soul into the body that'd been growing inside, something Egoei had planned ahead and prepared for years.

He doesn't bother explaining any of this to Elion, underscoring how little Egoei actually cares about him or anyone but himself.
