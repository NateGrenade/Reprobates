Revenant is a morally complex antagonist defined by profound hypocrisy: he genocided nearly all of the Atheri because he believed their immortality bred cruelty, yet retained his own power. He rationalizes this through a belief in his own indispensability to galactic peace, while fundamentally being driven by fear of death.

**Rule and Ideology**
Revenant rules with an iron fist through fear and decisive action, framing his imposed peace — maintained partly through something like mutually-assured-destruction leverage — as his highest good.

**Relationship with Morpheus**
Revenant and Morpheus were once close, something like best friends, before Revenant purged the Atheri. Revenant personally left Morpheus blinded and mortally wounded during that purge, though Morpheus survived and escaped.

**Death**
During Elion's rage-fueled assault on Revenant's capital, Revenant restrains and chokes Elion, delivering a long speech justifying his imposed peace and challenging Elion to explain how his own violence and rage differ from every past self-proclaimed savior who ended up believing the world belonged to them. Elion breaks down and admits his real motive isn't justice but pure hatred and revenge for everything Revenant took from him — the moment his self-image as a righteous avenger collapses, leaving Morpheus's nihilistic worldview as the only one left standing in his head. Elion's rage then boils over, destroying an entire city; even after his own powers burn out he keeps beating a near-death Revenant with his bare hands. Morpheus then steps in, uses the dying Revenant to locate his bio-engineering lab and the Aethercrux, and personally kills him.

**Ordering Note**
In an early personal organizing concept Nathan calls "The Four Men," the main cast is numbered by thematic/chronological order: Revenant (1st), Morpheus (2nd), Soren (3rd), Elion (4th).
