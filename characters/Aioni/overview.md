Aioni is a morally complex antagonist defined by profound hypocrisy: he genocided nearly all of the Atheri because he believed their immortality bred cruelty, yet retained his own power. He rationalizes this through a belief in his own indispensability to galactic peace, while fundamentally being driven by fear of death.

**Rule and Ideology**
Aioni rules with an iron fist through fear and decisive action, framing his imposed peace — maintained through brutal measures and even, at times, mass genocide — as his highest good.

**Relationship and backstory with Egoei**
Aioni and Egoei were once best friends about a million years before the beginning of the story. The two were prodigies even among the Atheri, respected and seen as the crown jewls of their generation. Despite their young age at the time, they worked together and successfully created the Aethercrux. It was a device unparalleled by any prior to it, magnifying the Atheri's power even further. It had the unique ability, among many others, to grant eternal life to whomever the user pleased. That said, all the power of the Aethercrux was generated through the sacrifice of other mortal beings, making the device itself inherently unethical and destructive.
Aioni was indifferent about the relatively small sacrifices required for the Aethercrux, and delighted with his success at first. But as thousands of years passed, he saw that the now-immortal Atheri became bored with their own lives. A sort of deep existential dread settled on the people over time, breeding ever more cruel ways to stay "entertained" and to fill their time. The Atheri had already not been too concerned about so-called "lesser" people, but eventually, they began torturing, enslaving, and "playing" with their lives as a source of vain laughter and enjoyment.

Aioni and Egoei were both horrified. Egoei held to his ideals and beleived they could change their people, direct their power to mutual good, etc. Aioni was less optimistic. He realized that eternal life and infinite power bred detached monsters, and he decided that the Atheri as a whole were a perpetual force of evil - disrupting peace, bringing death, and ontologically incapable of good or change. Aioni used the Aethercrux's power, fueled by billions of souls he deemed necessary sacrifices, and personally murdered every living Atheri save for a small few, who escaped to the edges of the cosmos. Egoei tried to stop Aioni, persuade him to give his people another chance.

Aioni personally left Egoei blinded and mortally wounded during that altercation, and Egoei barely survived and escaped.

From then on, Aioni decided to personally hunt down any remaining Atheri, including Egoei. But during this time, he saw that death, war, and cruelty didn't fade in the galaxy even with the Atheri dead. His philosophy of keeping the peace through power and brutal measures developed further, and Aioni eventually established a ruling empire that grew over countless millennia until it spanned a massive portion of the galaxy, which is the point at which we begin the story of Reprobates.

**Death**
During Elion's rage-fueled assault on Aioni's capital, Aioni restrains and chokes Elion, delivering a long speech justifying his imposed peace and challenging Elion to explain how his own violence and rage differ from every past self-proclaimed savior who ended up believing the world belonged to them. Elion breaks down and admits his real motive isn't justice but pure hatred and revenge for everything Aioni took from him — the moment his self-image as a righteous avenger collapses, leaving Egoei's nihilistic worldview as the only one left standing in his head. Elion's rage then boils over, destroying an entire city; even after his own powers burn out he keeps beating a near-death Aioni with his bare hands. Egoei then steps in, uses the dying Aioni to locate his bio-engineering lab and the Aethercrux, and personally kills him as Aioni begs for life - exposing Aioni's true hypocritical fear of death despite justifying such a fate for trillions in the past for peace.
