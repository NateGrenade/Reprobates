Aioni is a morally complex antagonist defined by profound hypocrisy: he genocided nearly all of the Atheri because he believed their immortality bred cruelty, yet retained his own power. He rationalizes this through a belief in his own indispensability to galactic peace, while fundamentally being driven by fear of death.

**Rule and Ideology**
Aioni rules with an iron fist through fear and decisive action, framing his imposed peace — maintained partly through something like mutually-assured-destruction leverage — as his highest good.

**Relationship with Egoei**
Aioni and Egoei were once close, something like best friends, before Aioni purged the Atheri. Aioni personally left Egoei blinded and mortally wounded during that purge, though Egoei survived and escaped.

**Death**
During Elion's rage-fueled assault on Aioni's capital, Aioni restrains and chokes Elion, delivering a long speech justifying his imposed peace and challenging Elion to explain how his own violence and rage differ from every past self-proclaimed savior who ended up believing the world belonged to them. Elion breaks down and admits his real motive isn't justice but pure hatred and revenge for everything Aioni took from him — the moment his self-image as a righteous avenger collapses, leaving Egoei's nihilistic worldview as the only one left standing in his head. Elion's rage then boils over, destroying an entire city; even after his own powers burn out he keeps beating a near-death Aioni with his bare hands. Egoei then steps in, uses the dying Aioni to locate his bio-engineering lab and the Aethercrux, and personally kills him.

**Ordering Note**
In an early personal organizing concept Nathan calls "The Four Men," the main cast is numbered by thematic/chronological order: Aioni (1st), Egoei (2nd), Soren (3rd), Elion (4th).
