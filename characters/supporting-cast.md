**Chara** — Elion and Soren's frail, sickly 11-year-old sister, and a moral mirror to both brothers. She dies; her decline is triggered by Elion ignoring a mugger she tried to stop, which fuels Elion's search for the Obelisk relic. Her death (and the desire to resurrect her) is later tied to the temptation Morpheus and the Aethercrux represent for Elion.

**Evelyn & Aeros** — Mortal companions in Arc 1. Aeros's suffering triggers Elion's Awakening ("Being Called").

**Dioikitis & Michani** — Arc 1 antagonists who lead the Relic Hunters. Dioikitis is a pragmatist who views relics as harvestable resources; Michani is a tech/analyst who humiliates Elion using combat data.

**The Companions** — In Arcs 3–5, Elion travels with an Aurelian survivor and a Galactic companion (either an Imperial defector or a stunted Atheri clone). They abandon Elion in Arc 5 when his morality darkens under Morpheus's influence, and refuse to fully welcome him back by the story's end.

**The Indifferentist Bounty Hunter** — Appears in Arcs 3–4. Has died thousands of times via his own power and believes the universe is cold, caring only about survival in the moment. His ability is a form of quantum/temporal cloning: he splits the timeline for 1–5 seconds into clones and solidifies whichever branch does the most damage, but experiences the deaths of all the failed timelines — an experience that broke his mind into apathetic nihilism. Elion defeats him by sapping his own Ki, letting himself be impaled, and using hyper-regeneration to crush/sever the hunter's arm — then stopping himself from finishing the hunter off in a rage to go save locals instead, shattering the hunter's indifferent worldview.

**Legion** — An insane enforcer serving Revenant who delights in destroying planets and inflicting pain. Elion eventually kills him, but the kill leaves Elion empty rather than satisfied.
