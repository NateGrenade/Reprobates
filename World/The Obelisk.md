## Overview
The Obelisk is a legendary relic built to heal ailments of all kinds. Most commoners consider it a myth. It was buried centuries ago and has not been activated in centuries.

## Location and surfacing
- It lies in a vast cavern beneath and around [[The Concord]]'s capital, reached through cave systems.
- Visible **rivers of Ki** (something between water and pure electricity, shifting purple, white, blue, and green) flow across an area over a mile wide, all pointing into the Obelisk.
- The Obelisk floats on gathered Ki, pressed against the cavern ceiling, trying to surface.
- Ki flows toward it fairly evenly from a huge radius, so the earthquakes have no single epicenter, and seismic data alone can't locate it. The Ki density saturates Sensitives' perception, so the RHS can't find it either. Aeros finds it by feeling the direction of flow.
- The earthquakes have grown for about 21 years, matching the twins' age (see [[Book 1 Plot Holes and Fixes]] §6.1).

## Healing
- **The Heart Gem:** a green gem at the center of the geometric design at the top. Torn out, it holds enough residual Ki to heal one person, but doing so drains it and renders the Obelisk useless.
- **Activation:** a user draws **energizing Ki runes** in a circle. Ki is channeled through the runes into the structure, the Heart Gem leaks a glowing green liquid through every seam, and after a white-out flash a beam of green energy heals whoever is in the circle, regrowing flesh and bone.
- **It cannot resurrect.** Aeros in Book 1 is near-dead, not dead. Bringing back the dead is unique to the [[Aethercrux]].
- Activating it makes the Ki rivers rise and the cavern collapse as the Obelisk fights to surface.

## Ki rivers
Falling into a Ki river is agonizing and burns body and soul layer by layer; it can kill. It is also where Elion's half-awakening happens (see [[Book 1 Ending]]).
