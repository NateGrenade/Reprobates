## Overview
Priests are [[Relic Hunters' Society|Relic Hunters]] who have ascended to rank XXVI or higher. Few ranks lie above them. This is the threshold where a member begins to learn about and understand the darker parts of the RHS.

## Initiation
Priests are initiated through gruesome, disgusting rituals that force them to go against their very nature, first as moral agents and then as humans. What this means varies between Priests, but it often involves disfigurement, engaging in sexual abuse, sacrifice, or other sickening rites.

## Identity
- Priests wear animal masks and call themselves only "Priest," with no identification beyond the mask. They never say "a priest": all of them treat it as a shared title and name.
- Beneath their masks they have disfigured themselves so they can never return to their former identity.
- Where Dioikitis's loyalty and purpose is to the RHS alone, Priests have removed any regard for life beyond the organization and their religion. They care nothing even for themselves.
- Many have had brain surgeries that stunt their capacity to feel fear or self-preservation, which makes them rather strange folk.
- Priests waged war on [[Aurelia]] with the [[Titans]].

## Dioikitis
[[Supporting Cast#Dioikitis & Michani|Dioikitis]] was on the cusp of becoming a Priest. He doesn't know of the rituals or the depth of the RHS's secrets, only whispers. Nathan intends to make this clearer in Book 1 edits.
