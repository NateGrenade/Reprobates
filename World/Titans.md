## Overview
Titans are amalgamations of relics and technology of old that [[Priests]] used to wage war on [[Aurelia]]. They are shaped to appear like strange humanoid animals: owls, cats, cows, eagles, bears, and the like.

## Legacy
- The war left Aurelia scarred and recovering for decades.
- Nobody knows the [[Relic Hunters' Society]] created the Titans. Many fear them like demigods, and myths have grown up around them.
- Some believe they were angels sent by God to punish mankind. The Church on Aurelia rejects this and regards them as a demonic force.
- Mentioning them is taboo, almost an omen. Making light of the events around them is especially distasteful.

## Open
- When the war happened and how it ended.
- Why an organization hiding from [[Aioni]] would wage open war. One proposed answer: the war broke the old order so the RHS could steer the founding of [[The Concord]] (see [[Concord Political Structure#The Titan war and the founding]]).
