## The Aethercrux
The Aethercrux is an ancient artifact created by both [[Aioni]] and [[Egoei]] working together. It is the strongest relic and THING in the entire galaxy. In exchange for sacrificing souls to it, it can generate immense power. Its power is so great that it can do almost any feat of raw power. It also has the unique power to immortalize the soul or even bring lost souls back to life. It is the final Macguffin, if you will, of the series that serves as the pinnacle symbol of individual greed, desire, and moral compromise - since activating it directly requires the death of others to serve your own purposes. Using it directly asserts that your desires are the only thing that matter to you in that moment, regardless of the fate/desires/good of others.

After Aioni used it to massacre the old Atheri about 1 million years ago, it was lost in the battle between Aioni and Egoei. Since the Aethercrux thrives off of souls, it almost has a pseudo-consciousness - leading it to subtly manipulate fate and those around it so that those most driven by their desires find it. Those driven by desire are more likely to offer it souls, which exposes why this thing is only pseudo-conscious. It may do seemingly intelligent things to fulfill its end, but being a cold machine with one purpose - to consume - it has no deeper "feelings", "thoughts", or "ideals" than simply that prime directive. It fits into a broader motif across the story of "The Good of the Created vs. The Privation/Lack of the Artificial". This motif will be expanded upon more later as the story develops more granularity.

In the final fight, [[Elion]] wins by destroying the Aethercrux rather than using it to fulfill his own desires (such as resurrecting [[Supporting Cast#Chara|Chara]]) or to defeat his enemies, sacrificing his eyes, divinity, and power to do so. [[Soren]], by contrast, dies trying to draw more power from the Aethercrux — his soul is consumed by it.

## In-world legend
In Book 1, the scholarly-posturing series *Relics in Antiquity* calls the Aethercrux "all powerful" and "a gift forged by God Himself." Elion dismisses it as a fairy tale. This is deliberate foreshadowing for [[Egoei]] (*Ego eimi*, "I am"): the legend is less inaccurate than it looks, since to almost any being in the galaxy an Atheri over a million years old might as well be God.
