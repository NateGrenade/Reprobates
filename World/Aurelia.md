## Aurelia
Aurelia is the home planet of [[Elion]], [[Soren]], and [[Supporting Cast#Chara|Chara]]. It is destroyed in Arc 3, which expands the scope of the setting beyond it.

Most of Aurelia runs on a medieval level of technology plus magic: [[Seals|seals]], and the occasional relic or mechanism people get their hands on, which lets them do some pretty wacky stuff. Elion, "being basically a medieval dude," can't identify a blinking tracking device. The [[Relic Hunters' Society]]'s advanced technology is not native to Aurelia.

Trilogy One (Books 1–3) takes place on Aurelia, mostly in [[The Concord]].

## History and society
- A war waged by the [[Titans]] left Aurelia scarred and recovering for decades. Mentioning them is taboo.
- The Church on Aurelia regards the Titans as a demonic force, rejecting the belief that they were angels sent to punish mankind.
- The [[Relic Hunters' Society]] covertly controls most of Aurelia's governments (see [[Relic Hunters' Society#Grip on Aurelia]]).
