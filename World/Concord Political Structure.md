## Overview
[[The Concord]] is a decentralized array of cities and kingdoms unified under one name. The name does the work: a *concord* is an agreement, and the Concord is literally a compact between High Houses under a Crown. The Crown doesn't micromanage cities or regions.

## Tiers
- **The Crown** (king or emperor, undecided) rules the capital directly and commands the Elite Concord Guard. It settles disputes between houses, keeps the compact's few laws, and calls the houses together against outside threats, like the "barbarians" beyond Ahafurt's wall. It doesn't govern the cities.
- **High Houses** rule a whole city or region. Inside their territory they're almost entirely autonomous: taxes, law, levies, and walls are theirs. They're often in fierce competition, yet carry respect for one another under a shared code, and they defer and bow to the Crown. [[Elion]]'s family is a High House commanding the whole region/city of Ahafurt.
- **Lesser houses** hold districts, towns, and estates within a High House's region and are sworn to it. Their loyalty is a High House's real power base, and rivals can try to win them away. Peoples below them may hold further subdivisions.

## Rivalry short of war
Houses may compete in everything except open war against each other. Open war breaks the Concord and brings in the Guard. So rivalry moves into prestige, trade, marriages, duels, intrigue, and heirloom [[Seals|seals]], which families hand down.
- Ahafurt's monthly demonstrations of power do political work: they show strength to rival houses and to the house's own lesser houses.

## Heirs
A High House with no heir risks losing its seat to the Crown, its rivals, or its own vassals. This explains the adoption urgency in [[Elion and Soren Origin]], why the parents grip the title so tightly, and it bears on the "true heir" question in [[Book 1 Plot Holes and Fixes]].

## The RHS and the compact
A decentralized realm is easy to control covertly. The [[Relic Hunters' Society]] only needs the Crown and its rune network (see [[Relic Hunters' Society#Grip on Aurelia]]), plus houses busy competing so they never unite. The rivalry is itself one of the distractions.
- The Book 3 collapse ([[Concord Collapse Return]]: martial law, rebellions) reads as the compact coming apart when the RHS's grip slips.

## The Titan war and the founding
The Concord was founded after the [[Titans]]' war, when the houses banded together, and the RHS steered its founding. This gives the war a purpose: break the old order and install one the RHS can control. It also answers why an organization hiding from [[Aioni]] would risk open war.
