## Overview
The Concord (the Aurelian Concord) is the nation on [[Aurelia]] where [[Elion]] and [[Soren]] live, and the main setting of Trilogy One. Its government is secretly controlled by the [[Relic Hunters' Society]] (see [[Relic Hunters' Society#Grip on The Concord]]). Locals speak Concordian.

## The capital
- The seat of government, enforced by the **Elite Concord(ian) Guard**. Elion won't openly defy it, unlike the illegitimate RHS.
- Magic is all the rage here: even the lower class would be "upper crust" to most outsiders.
- [[The Obelisk]] has been surfacing beneath it, causing earthquakes for about 21 years. In Book 1 the quakes turn erratic and immense, and the city goes into lockdown (residents may leave; no one may enter) while citizens flee.
- Something about the city's atmosphere is deeply wrong: it makes Elion nauseous.

## Ahafurt
- The city ruled by Elion and Soren's noble family; they live in its manor.
- A towering wall faces outward against "barbarians." Its wealth shows in streetlamps lit even in the poor district, which Elion considers a waste.
- Elion and Soren practically govern it without having inherited it (see [[Book 1 Beginning]]).
- The family stages monthly public demonstrations of power to show "the might and wealth of the bloodline."
- An RHS sanctuary lies beneath it (Dioikitis's post).

## Maristadiz
- A large coastal city and a center of trade and commerce, about four days' courier relay from Ahafurt.
- It hosts the RHS's Maristadiz Survey Network HQ, hidden in a rundown back-alley building, and a sanctuary with a master regional terminal. Part of the sanctuary collapsed in Book 1.
