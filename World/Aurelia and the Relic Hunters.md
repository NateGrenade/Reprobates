## Aurelia
Aurelia is the home planet of [[Elion]], [[Soren]], and [[Supporting Cast#Chara|Chara]]. It is destroyed in Arc 2, which expands the scope of the setting beyond it. Aurelia is a planet that operates on a medieval level of technology, aside from the relics (magic powered items) they use in day-to-day life and in combat. 

## The Relic Hunters
The Relic Hunters' Society is a religious/pirate faction that worships ancient Atheri magic. They have some structural parallels to the Freemasons, especially in their secretive nature and their ranking system. They excavate relics while blending into local populations, valuing relics even over lives. They serve as the antagonist faction for the first three major arcs. Their Arc 1 leaders are [[Supporting Cast#Dioikitis & Michani|Dioikitis]] (a pragmatist and arrogant man, surpassing and mirroring Elion's disposition in arc 1) and Michani (a tech/analyst who takes after his leader in arrogance, but leans slightly sadistic).
