## Overview
The Relic Hunters' Society (RHS) is a religious/pirate faction that worships ancient Atheri magic. It has structural parallels to the Freemasons, especially its secrecy and ranking system. It excavates relics while blending into local populations, valuing relics even over lives, and hides them away for private worship, veneration, and experimentation.

The RHS is not native to [[Aurelia]]: they are basically space aliens with super-tech, hiding out on Aurelia (among other planets) to remain undiscovered. They speak their own language among themselves (in-lore the Relic Hunter language; Coptic in reality, see [[Book 1 Plot Holes and Fixes]] §6.4). Dioikitis calls the locals "worthless Aurelians."

They are the antagonist faction for Arcs 1–4.

## Faith: Anokpe
The RHS swear by and worship the god **Anokpe**, who is actually [[Egoei]]: Anokpe is Coptic for "I am," the same phrase "Egoei" comes from in Greek (*ego eimi*). Almost no one in the organization knows Egoei as a physical being; they conceive of him mythically and metaphysically, the way an Abrahamic religion might. Egoei set the RHS up for his own purposes, but it is not literally puppeted by him, and nearly all members have no idea of his Atheri roots or what it all really means. (Further, speculative history: [[Egoei and the Relic Hunters]].)

## Two layers
- **Surface institution** (where Dioikitis operates): protects the faith, guards its secrets, and avoids [[Aioni]]'s wrath. Officers like Dioikitis know some of the deeper and darker practices, know Anokpe, and know enough about the outside galaxy (e.g. Aioni annihilating worlds where Atheri are spotted) to fear [[Rune Eyes]], without fully understanding them or what an "Atheri" is.
- **Deeper layer**: knows more, but not everything. Dioikitis and Michani don't scratch the surface of how deep the organization goes. Michani's place is undecided; he possibly reaches the deeper layer. See [[Supporting Cast#Dioikitis & Michani]].

When Rune Eyes surface, the surface layer's priority is that no one learns of them. In Book 1, Dioikitis keeps Soren's existence from even other RHS members and orders every witness killed.

## Members and ranks
- Members are recruited from childhood, and many are young. Teams form very strong bonds.
- Sensitives are tracked (through lineage) and conscripted, including women, who serve under duress. Members must cut their hair.
- Rank numerals run at least to XV (server wings need XV or higher). Officers lead teams of four members. Elite strike squads exist (Dioikitis's four-person squad).
- Robes have gray accents; Sensitive-led survey teams wear blue. The team ID is printed on the **left** sleeve. Members use an RHS salute.
- The organization is a bloated bureaucracy: leaving a post or assembling a task force needs approval from higher-ups.

## Technology
- Elite powered suits (a lightweight battle suit with wires, metal panels, and lights) that enhance strength and speed, with thrusters/jet flight, IR vision, and overdrive (risking overheating). Plasma wrist and hand cannons. Techno-staffs that extend and carry electricity. Invisibility seals.
- Tracking chips that burrow under the skin, feed off the host's Ki, shock the host when they draw Ki, and are likely rigged against tampering (see [[Elion]]).
- Relics and mechanisms with embedded seals (a flail with an extending chain; a flamethrower).
- Michani is half-mechanical.

## Sanctuaries and data network
- Hidden underground sanctuaries. Entrances and exits are always shifting, and none is both at once. A destroyed door triggers a lockdown that seals the entrance corridor in several feet of stone.
- Entrance-credential seals grant access by rank. Members destroy their own if a fight is unwinnable. Escorts submit the escorted members' details and hold their seals inside higher-access areas.
- Survey data flows from teams to regional checkpoints and is carried by couriers node to node, on fixed check-in times, to hubs like the Maristadiz Survey Network.
- Data is split across many **master regional terminals (MRTs)** across the Concord and beyond. **True master terminals (TMTs)** deep inside sanctuaries compile them, so no single terminal holds everything.
- A **Teleportation Seal Matrix** moves personnel between sanctuaries (Ahafurt → Maristadiz in Book 1).

## Grip on The Concord
*Planned. v1 doesn't hint at this yet, and Nathan intends to add it.*
- The RHS controls [[The Concord]]'s government from the shadows, using relics, mechanisms, and political leverage to magically and politically suppress serious dissent.
- It has the government keep people entertained and occupied with meaningless things.
- People are allowed to complain about the RHS, which makes lulling them into submission with magic and propaganda easier: a person who can complain feels righteous, free, and productive, even if they never act.
- Because of their biology and powers, [[Elion]] and [[Soren]] are barely affected by these spells, so they can express anger at the RHS more deliberately and actively.

This is why the RHS is openly resented but tolerated. The Book 1 draft currently overstates how hated they are; the Book 3 scene prototype understates it.
