# Arc 1 Audit: v1 draft vs. vault

**Source:** [v1 (Drive, Arc 1 folder)](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1dNZ1lsFW68FRPvj49GyVS3dcCaiqfpJbBBf295Xxe0o/edit), read in full as of 2026-10-22, including the "TOTALLY DEPRICATED" block at the end.
**Compared against:** every vault note that touches Arc 1, plus character, power, and world notes.

## How to use this
1. Work through the items and tick one verdict box on each. Add a note wherever the answer is "it depends" or needs nuance.
2. Then tell Claude something like *"apply the Arc 1 audit."* Claude will update the **vault only**, for items where v1 wins or you wrote a merge. It never edits v1. Where the vault wins, fixing v1 is left to you.
3. Unticked items are left alone.

## Labels
**Degree** (how loudly they disagree):
- **Superseded**: v1 explicitly replaces or deprecates what the vault says.
- **Explicit**: both state incompatible things outright.
- **Apparent**: reads as a contradiction on a plain read, but a small assumption could reconcile it.
- **Implicit**: neither states a conflict, but one implies something the other rules out.

**Severity** (how much it matters to the story):
- 🔴 **Foundational**: theme, philosophy, a core character arc, a power-system rule, or a plot beat other events depend on.
- 🟠 **Major**: a significant plot or character fact with knock-on effects.
- 🟡 **Moderate**: shapes a scene or character, but doesn't ripple far.
- ⚪ **Minor**: details, terminology, flavor.

**Read**: my guess at whether the v1 version is a *deliberate evolution* (emphasized, rewritten, clearly intended) or *likely accidental* (subtle, incidental, probably written without the vault in mind). It's only a guess, so overrule freely.

### F1 · What triggers Elion's Being Called
**Degree:** Explicit · **Severity:** 🔴 Foundational · **Read:** deliberate evolution (part of the rewritten ending)

- **Vault**
  - [[Supporting Cast#Evelyn & Aeros]]: "Aeros's suffering triggers Elion's Awakening ("Being Called")." [[Structure and Themes]] says the same.
  - [[Arc 1 Ending]]: Elion's drowning monologue turns on Aeros and Evelyn "laughing together… true siblings," his broken promise to them, and Soren fighting "to the point of collapse." His turn is outward-facing.
- **v1** (Climax → End)
  - Aeros is killed right before this, but the river monologue is about Chara and himself: the planche/candle memory ("You're also trying really really hard"), then *"I can't let myself be weak… Since when does trying matter."*

- **Why it matters:** this sets the moral color of Elion's awakening.

  - Both versions satisfy [[Being Called]]'s rule (a reason to live beyond self-preservation), via Chara.
