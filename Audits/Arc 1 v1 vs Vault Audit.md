# Arc 1 Audit: v1 draft vs. vault

**Source:** [v1 (Drive, Arc 1 folder)](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1dNZ1lsFW68FRPvj49GyVS3dcCaiqfpJbBBf295Xxe0o/edit), read in full as of 2026-10-22, including the "TOTALLY DEPRICATED" block at the end.
**Compared against:** every vault note that touches Arc 1, plus character, power, and world notes.

## How to use this
1. Work through the items and tick one verdict box on each. Add a note wherever the answer is "it depends" or needs nuance.
2. Then tell Claude something like *"apply the Arc 1 audit."* Claude will update the **vault only**, for items where v1 wins or you wrote a merge. It never edits v1. Where the vault wins, fixing v1 is left to you.
3. Unticked items are left alone.

## Labels
**Degree** (how loudly they disagree):
- **Superseded**: v1 explicitly replaces or deprecates what the vault says.
- **Explicit**: both state incompatible things outright.
- **Apparent**: reads as a contradiction on a plain read, but a small assumption could reconcile it.
- **Implicit**: neither states a conflict, but one implies something the other rules out.

**Severity** (how much it matters to the story):
- 🔴 **Foundational**: theme, philosophy, a core character arc, a power-system rule, or a plot beat other events depend on.
- 🟠 **Major**: a significant plot or character fact with knock-on effects.
- 🟡 **Moderate**: shapes a scene or character, but doesn't ripple far.
- ⚪ **Minor**: details, terminology, flavor.

**Read**: my guess at whether the v1 version is a *deliberate evolution* (emphasized, rewritten, clearly intended) or *likely accidental* (subtle, incidental, probably written without the vault in mind). It's only a guess, so overrule freely.

### F3 · Is Elion's adaptation controlled "from day one"?
**Degree:** Apparent · **Severity:** 🔴 Foundational (power-system rule) · **Read:** unclear; could be a deliberate "first awakening is wild" beat, or accidental drift

- **Why it matters:** if v1 wins, the rule needs an exception for the first awakening, or a rewrite. If the vault wins, the climax needs to show Elion *choosing* to let go.

**Verdict:**
- [x] v1 wins → update the vault
- [ ] Vault wins → I'll revise v1 myself
- [x] Merge / other (explain below)

**Your notes:**
This is a complex one because there's a harmonization here that isn't apparent in the vault, coupled with actual changes to the story. I overstated my original intent when the vault had that information stored. When I say Elion can control his adaptation, I mean that he can control when it begins and the degree to which it runs. It does not mean he's micromanaging it at all times or how it works. The more complex physical adaptations, like structuring miniature Ki lattices across/under his skin to resist thunder, is an adaptation driven by instinct but one that he could have cancelled if he chose. It's the same way that you're in "control" of how fast a car goes without being the one who generates the force of the motor, or who physically moves the car. 

That said, Elion DOES NOT have conscious control over his adaptation's activation just yet, at least not in any complete way. This is more of a plot point that will be elaborated on in book 2, but it's worth noting so more contradictions don't appear later. To keep my claim precise: Elion's control is about degree, not function, and it also only arrives once Elion has fully awakened his Rune Eyes, something that has not technically happened yet (see the previous point)

- **Vault** ([[Soren Character Arc]]): the order is **Conviction → Suffering → Despair → Power → Radicalization**. *"Only after reaching this point [total despair] does Soren realize… he actually has the power to change things."*

- **Why it matters:** the arc note says the realization comes after despair; v1 seeds power and entitlement in Arc 1, before any despair. **Verdict:**
- [ ] v1 wins → update the vault
- [ ] Vault wins → I'll revise v1 myself
- [x] Merge / other (explain below)

**Your notes:**
The order given for Soren in the vault is referring to a broader character arc, but it doesn't literally preclude Soren from having power prior to his despair. In fact, the line you quote even says this: Soren doesn't gain power after despairing, he realizes he already has it. The Rune Eyes, however, massively increase in strength as Soren's convictions harden, which makes the power he gains post-despair formidable. The power Soren displays in v1 is great relative to what we've seen so far, but is laughable in the face of cosmic conflicts, cycles of war and hate, etc. So neither is "wrong" but Obsidian's wording should probably be cleaned up to make this clear.
