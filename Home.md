## Status key
Every note has a `status` and a one-line `description` in its properties.
- **canon**: settled
- **provisional**: intended canon, still open to revision
- **proposal**: a concrete idea under consideration
- **speculation**: exploratory, what-if
- **rejected**: discarded; kept for reference
