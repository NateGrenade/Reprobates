**The Aethercrux**
The Aethercrux is a soul-immortality artifact hidden by Revenant alongside a machine capable of creating new Atheri bodies. It has no power of its own — it derives power only from consuming/sacrificing others' souls to grant the user's wishes. It's more directly inspired by the Eliacube from Wakfu than any equivalent from Naruto.

Morpheus's soul (not his body) is bound to the Aethercrux via an ancient deal for soul-immortality, struck after a confrontation with Revenant millions of years ago — which is why destroying the Aethercrux later kills him permanently, rather than allowing another resurrection.

In the final fight, Elion wins by destroying the Aethercrux rather than using it to fulfill his own desires (such as resurrecting Chara) or to defeat his enemies, sacrificing his eyes, divinity, and power to do so. Soren, by contrast, dies trying to draw more power from the Aethercrux — his soul is consumed by it.

**The Zenith (Sword of Grace)**
The Zenith is Elion's ultimate weapon: a soul-sword capable of cutting through absolute rules and leaving permanent wounds on immortals. Manifesting it requires Elion to permanently burn out both his Rune Eyes and his right arm.
