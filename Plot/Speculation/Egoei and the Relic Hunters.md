# Egoei and the Relic Hunters

**Status: SPECULATION**

The [[Relic Hunters' Society|Relic Hunters]] were established by [[Egoei]] for his own purposes (to sustain and worship him, among other things), although this connection is not revealed during Arcs 2–3. He doesn't hands-on control or puppet the organization. It's split into a surface institution and a deeper layer that knows more, though not everything, and nearly all members know their god Anokpe only mythically, never as an Atheri (see [[Relic Hunters' Society#Two layers]] and [[Relic Hunters' Society#Faith: Anokpe]]).

Their hidden purpose is to sustain Egoei after the destruction of his [[Rune Eyes]]. The destruction of the Rune Eyes would cause his physical body and vitality to degrade over time, requiring an external means of maintaining his corporeal existence.

The [[Aethercrux]] sustains Egoei's soul; child sacrifice sustains his body. The Relic Hunters serve as the institution through which those sacrifices are obtained and carried out.

This would give the Relic Hunters a deeper connection to Egoei than is apparent during their early appearances. Their religious identity and obsession with relics can remain genuine within the organization while concealing their ultimate function in preserving Egoei.

The exact history of the Relic Hunters' founding, the mechanism by which sacrifice sustains Egoei's body, the degree of direct control Egoei exercises over the organization, and the circumstances of the Aethercrux's role remain speculative.

Egoei's present-day state is itself unsettled; see [[Egoei#Open questions]].
