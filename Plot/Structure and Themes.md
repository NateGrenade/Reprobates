## Central Theme: The Cross vs. The Throne
This is the thematic label for the story's central conflict. [[Soren]] seeks the Throne: mechanical, calculated top-down control meant to eliminate suffering, which instead breeds tyranny. [[Elion]] embraces the Cross: stepping into the mud, absorbing the world's brokenness, and enduring others' suffering unilaterally.

## "The Four Men"
An early personal organizing concept numbering the main cast by thematic/chronological order: [[Aioni]] (1st), [[Egoei]] (2nd), Soren (3rd), Elion (4th).

## Very Most Basic Structure
One arc = one book. Trilogy One (Books 1–3) is the Aurelia trilogy.
- Arc 1 (Book 1, *Reprobates: The Obelisk*): Elion and Soren search for [[The Obelisk]] to save [[Supporting Cast#Chara|Chara]], running afoul of the [[Relic Hunters' Society|Relic Hunters]]' Society, whose Book 1 face is [[Supporting Cast#Dioikitis & Michani|Dioikitis]]'s squad (with Michani). Soren fully awakens his Rune Eyes; Elion half-awakens (see [[Book 1 Ending]]).
- Arc 2 (Book 2): investigating the Relic Hunters and discovering their dark side. Elion fully awakens late in the book.
- Arc 3 (Book 3): the collapse of [[Aurelia|Aurelia]], Elion's home planet, which is destroyed, expanding the scope of the setting.
- Arcs 4–6: Elion travels with his Companions/friends (an Aurelian survivor and a Galactic companion) and faces the [[Supporting Cast#The Indifferentist Bounty Hunter|Indifferentist Bounty Hunter]] and [[Supporting Cast#Legion|Legion]], both serving Aioni. The Companions abandon Elion in Arc 6 when his morality darkens under Egoei's influence.
- End of Arc 6: Elion's rage-fueled assault on Aioni's capital destroys an entire city and ends with Aioni near death. Egoei then personally kills Aioni to obtain what he needs (locating the bio-engineering lab and the [[Aethercrux]]) and kills himself on the battlefield to be resurrected — without explaining any of this to Elion. Aioni's imposed peace collapses into faction warfare immediately after.
- Arc 7: A galactic civil war, incited by Elion's own mistakes, which he ultimately fails to stop by the series' end.

*Note the above is VERY simplified. For details just ask questions or, if available, refer to dedicated arc files.

## Trilogy One and the Aioni thread
- Splitting the old Arc 2 into Books 2 and 3 gives the pacing room to dramatize Aurelia's fall without it competing with the Relic Hunters investigation, and gives relationships between characters longer to build.
- **Detection:** when Elion and Soren first awaken in Book 1 (Soren fully, Elion half), Aioni becomes aware that Atheri exist somewhere. He can't locate them, because their Ki is inhibited by a **suppression seal**.
- **Location:** when Elion fully awakens late in Book 2, he breaks the suppression seal inhibiting his Ki. Aioni can then locate Elion and Soren, and he sends [[Supporting Cast#Legion|Legion]] after them. Legion arrives at the end of Book 3. (This replaces the old end-of-Book-1 epilogue, in which someone in deep space senses two Atheri and summons Legion; that scene effectively moves to Book 2.)
- The [[Relic Hunters' Society]]'s terror of Rune Eyes comes from knowing Aioni annihilates worlds where Atheri are spotted.

## Ending
Elion ends the main series at a material, physical, and relational low — having lost nearly everything, including relationships with old companions who reject him — while still holding to his convictions and continuing to serve, expecting nothing in return. His final action in the series is thinking to himself "I need to end this war" as he walks off towards his ship. No explicit divine reward or sign of cosmic vindication is given (see [[Being Called]] for the reasoning behind that choice).
